## Four kinds of certification

| Certification | What the notary proves | Typical document | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Date** ({{дата}}) | That the document was presented on that date | A contract that needs a certain date | [[gpk:589:1]] |
| **Signature** ({{подпис}}) | That this person signed (or confirmed the signature) before the notary | Power of attorney for a bank, declaration, consent for a child to travel, vehicle sale contract | [[gpk:589:2]] |
| **Content** ({{съдържание}}) | The content of the document at the time of certification; a copy stays in the archive | Long documents that must stay unchanged | [[gpk:589:1]], [[gpk:590:2]] |
| **Signature and content at the same time** | Both, in one act, with one copy in the archive | Power of attorney to sell real estate, transfer of company shares, marriage contract | [[gpk:590:4]] |
| **Copy** ({{препис}}) | That the copy matches the original or another copy | Copy of a diploma, a deed, a passport | [[gpk:591]] |

All certifications are made by an **endorsement** ({{удостоверителен надпис}}) on the document itself. The rules of [[gpk:580]] on the content of a deed apply, as far as there are no special rules ([[gpk:590:1]]). After the certification, the private documents go back to the persons who presented them ([[gpk:590:3]]).

## Certification of a signature

### The rule

The persons whose signatures are certified **must appear personally** before the notary and either **sign before the notary** or **confirm** signatures already made ([[gpk:589:2]]). For signatures the rules on identity, capacity, illiterate persons, interpreters and witnesses apply ([[gpk:589:2]]).

**Documents about real estate.** When the document will be used to create, change or end rights in real estate, the persons must **write their full name** by hand and sign before the notary, or, if they signed before, write their full name and confirm the signature ([[gpk:589:2]]). The fee is also higher: 10 leva per signature ([[tarifa:5]]).

A breach of the rule of personal appearance makes the certification **null** ([[gpk:576]]).

### Step by step

1. **Ask what the document is for.** If it serves a deal that needs a special form, check that a signature certification is enough. A power of attorney to sell real estate needs signature **and content at the same time** ([[zzd:37]]).
2. **Read the document.** The notary must not certify documents against the law ([[gpk:574]]). Blank spaces in the text are crossed out before signing. Do not certify a signature on a blank sheet or on a document with missing essential parts.
3. **Check identity and capacity** (chapter 7).
4. **The person signs** before the notary or confirms the existing signature; for real estate documents, also writes the full name.
5. **Prepare the endorsement.** Most offices use stamps of the approved model ([[n32:17]]).
6. **Enter the act in the general register** ([[gpk:590:2]], [[n32:7:1]]). The register number goes into the endorsement.
7. **The notary signs and seals.** For documents of several sheets, the sheets are numbered and attached, every two sheets joined with signature and seal in at least two places ([[n32:20]]).
8. **Enter the extract in the information system** of the Notary Chamber when the document is a power of attorney for banks, vehicles, the tax agency, insurers, or a document for the Commercial Register ([[n32:9а]]).
9. **Issue the bill** ([[znnd:89]]) and hand back the document.

```doc Typical wording of a signature endorsement
Удостоверявам подписа върху този документ, положен от
Иван Петров Иванов, ЕГН 7501011234, л.к. № 640000000, изд. на 01.02.2020 г. от МВР – София.
Рег. № 1234 / 21.10.2026 г.
Такса ... евро
НОТАРИУС: ..........  (печат)
```

### Documents in a foreign language

If a private document is in a foreign language and is not subject to entry, the rule on interpreters applies accordingly ([[gpk:589:3]]). In practice the notary must understand the content to check its legality. When the notary does not know the language, the office asks for a translation or certifies only a bilingual document.

## Certification of content

A person may present a private document to have its **content** certified ([[gpk:589:1]]). The person presents a **copy** of the document; after the certification, the copy, duly certified, is filed in a special book ([[gpk:590:2]]), the book of invitations, protests, protocols and copies ([[znnd:28а:1]]).

## Signature and content at the same time

Several laws require the signature and the content to be certified **at the same time** ({{едновременно}}):

- a power of attorney for a deal that needs a notarial deed ([[zzd:37]]);
- a contract for the transfer of a company share ([[tz:129:2]]);
- a contract for the transfer of an enterprise ([[tz:15:1]]);
- a marriage contract ([[sk:39:1]]);
- protocols of certain decisions of an OOD ([[tz:137:4]]).

The applicant presents **two or more identical copies**. They are signed under the rules for signatures. After the certification, **one copy is filed in a special book**, and the others go to the applicant ([[gpk:590:4]]). Powers of attorney with signature and content certified at the same time are entered in the information system of the Notary Chamber **at once** ([[n32:9а]]).

> [!warn] One act, one moment
> "At the same time" means that the signature and the content are certified in one endorsement on the same day, before the same notary. A power of attorney with a signature certified in Varna and content certified a week later in Sofia does not meet [[zzd:37]].

## Certification of a date

A person may present a private document only to have the **date of presentation** certified ([[gpk:589:1]]). The date certification gives the document a **certain date** ({{достоверна дата}}) towards third persons. Example: a lease contract with a certain date binds a buyer of the property for up to one year after the purchase ([[zzd:237]]). Date and signature certifications are entered in a special register ([[gpk:590:2]]); the fee is charged once when date and signatures are certified together ([[tarifa:5]]).

## Certification of copies

When certifying a copy, the notary **compares the copy with the original** and states in the endorsement ([[gpk:591:1]]):

- **who presented** the document from which the copy was made;
- whether the copy was made **from the original** or **from another copy**;
- whether the document had **deletions, additions, corrections** or other peculiarities.

```doc Typical wording of a copy endorsement
Удостоверявам верността на този препис с оригинала на документа, представен ми от
Мария Георгиева Стоянова, ЕГН ..., без зачерквания, добавки, поправки и други особености.
Рег. № 1235 / 21.10.2026 г.
НОТАРИУС: ..........  (печат)
```

> [!tip] Copies of identity documents and diplomas
> Clients often ask for certified copies of passports, diplomas and birth certificates for use abroad. A certified copy has the same evidential force as the original **only** for copies of **official** documents certified by an official ([[gpk:179:2]]). Some foreign authorities require an apostille on the copy; the Ministry of Justice issues it for notarial certifications (chapter 5).

## The registers

- Certifications of **date and signatures**: an entry in the general register ([[gpk:590:2]], [[n32:7:1]]).
- Certifications of **content** and simultaneous certifications: a copy goes to the book of invitations, protests, protocols and copies, or, if the document is subject to entry, to the book of acts subject to entry ([[znnd:28а:1]]).
- Every day's entries are closed with the notary's signature and seal ([[n32:5:1]]).
