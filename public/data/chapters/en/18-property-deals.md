## What all real estate deeds share

Every deed in this chapter transfers, creates, changes or ends a real right in real estate. So the rules of chapters 13, 14 and 17 apply: the notary of the property's district ([[gpk:570:1]]), a written request ([[gpk:571]]), the ownership check ([[gpk:586]]), the public-debt check ([[dopk:264:1]]), the declarations ([[znnd:25:8]]), entry on the same day ([[znnd:25:5]]) and the notice to the municipality ([[zmdt:51:2]]). Only the notary personally issues these deeds; an assistant notary may not ([[znnd:40:1]]). This chapter covers what is **different**.

## Gift (дарение)

- A gift transfers the property **at once** and **free of charge**; the recipient accepts it ([[zzd:225]]). Both sign the deed.
- A promise of a gift has no effect; a gift of future property is void ([[zzd:226]]).
- A gift can be revoked only for the grounds in [[zzd:227]].
- Material interest for the fee: the market price, and at least the tax assessment ([[znnd:96:1]], [[znnd:96:3]]).

### The tax on gifts

| Relationship | Tax | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Relatives in the **direct line** (parents, children, grandchildren) and **spouses** | **Exempt** | [[zmdt:44:6]] |
| Brothers and sisters and their children | 0.4–0.8%, set by the municipality | [[zmdt:47:1]] |
| All other persons | 3.3–6.6%, set by the municipality | [[zmdt:47:1]] |

### Gifts to children

A child may **receive** a gift. A parent who **donates** to the child cannot also accept the gift in the child's name, because a representative may not contract with himself ([[zzd:38]]). The **other parent** accepts, or the court appoints a **special representative** when the interests conflict ([[sk:129:2]]). A minor can never **make** a gift ([[sk:130:4]]).

### Gift with a reserved right of use

Parents often donate the home to a child and reserve a **life-long right of use** for themselves, or for the surviving parent. The deed then states two things: the transfer of the bare ownership and the reservation of the right of use ([[zs:56]], [[zs:59]]). When the holder dies, the right of use ends by law; the owner simply presents the death certificate to the municipality and the registry office as needed.

> [!warn] Gifts and other heirs
> A gift to one child can affect the reserved portion of the other children. After the donor's death they may ask for the reduction of the gift ([[zn:30]], [[zn:33]]). The notary explains this risk, especially when the donor has several children.

## Exchange (замяна)

- The rules on sale apply: each party is the seller of what it gives and the buyer of what it receives ([[zzd:223]]).
- Material interest: the market price of the **more valuable** property ([[znnd:96:1]]).
- Local tax: on the property of **higher value**, paid by the party who acquires it, unless agreed otherwise ([[zmdt:45:1]], [[zmdt:47:2]]).
- If the properties are in different districts, each deed must meet the district rule; offices often do a separate deed in each district.

## Transfer against maintenance and care

An older person transfers a property to another person, who undertakes to **support** the transferor (food, clothing, medicine, money) and to **care** for him or her (company, help, nursing) for life ({{издръжка и гледане}}). The contract is not named in the Obligations and Contracts Act but is valid under the freedom of contract ([[zzd:9]]) and is entered like a sale ([[pv:4]]).

Key points for the deed:

- Describe the obligation clearly: what support, where the transferor will live, who provides care, what happens if the transferee is ill or abroad.
- The obligation is **personal** and lasts for life; its value is uncertain, which is why the contract is **aleatory** (it depends on how long the transferor lives).
- If the transferee does not perform, the transferor may **terminate** the contract **only through a court** ([[zzd:87]]). The claim is entered in the property register ([[pv:11]]).
- The material interest is the market price of the property ([[znnd:96:1]]). The local tax is the tax for onerous acquisition ([[zmdt:47:2]]).

> [!tip] Meet the transferor alone
> These contracts are a common tool of pressure on elderly people. The notary meets the transferor **alone** ([[znnd:20]]), checks that the transferor understands that ownership passes **now**, and explains how the contract can be ended. Chapter 27 lists the warning signs.

## Building right (право на строеж)

- The owner of land grants the right to build a building on it, and the builder becomes the owner of the building ([[zs:63]]). The right may be granted for a term ([[zs:65]]); it lapses if not used for **5 years** ([[zs:67]]).
- Material interest for the fee: **90%** of the market price of the land, or of the part on which the right is granted ([[znnd:96:2]]).
- The common deal with developers: the landowner grants a building right and, in exchange, receives finished apartments in the future building ({{обезщетение}}). The deed must state precisely which apartments, their area and position, the deadline, the quality of construction, and the penalties or guarantees if the developer is late.
- Rights to add floors or extensions to an existing building are a kind of building right ([[zs:66]]).

## Right of use (право на ползване)

- Created by a deed, or reserved in a sale or gift ([[zs:56]]).
- Material interest: the market rent for the whole term, and if no term is set, for **3 years** ([[znnd:96:2]]).
- It ends at the holder's death, or when the holder is a legal entity, when it ends; it also ends by non-use for 5 years or when the property perishes ([[zs:59]]).
- The holder may **waive** the right. A waiver of a real right in real estate is entered ([[pv:4]]); it needs at least a notarially certified signature.

## Voluntary partition (доброволна делба)

- Every co-owner and every co-heir may ask for partition at any time ([[zs:34]], [[zn:69:1]]).
- A voluntary partition of real estate needs written form with notarially certified signatures; persons without capacity or absent persons need court permission ([[zs:35]]). In practice the notary issues a partition contract under item 8 of the tariff ([[tarifa:8]]), and it is entered ([[pv:4]]).
- **All co-heirs** must take part; otherwise the partition is **entirely void** ([[zn:75:2]]).
- Material interest: the market price of **all the shares** ([[znnd:96:1]]).
- Local tax: only on the **increase** of a co-owner's share ([[zmdt:47:3]]).
- If one co-sharer must pay compensation to another, the creditor co-sharer has a **statutory mortgage** on the properties allotted to the debtor ([[zzd:168]]).

## Mortgage (ипотека)

### The contractual mortgage

- Created by **entry** in the property register on the basis of a contract or the law; only on individually identified properties and for a fixed sum ([[zzd:166]]).
- Made by **notarial deed** stating the creditor, the debtor, the owner (if different), the property, the secured claim and the amount ([[zzd:167]]). Any uncertainty about these persons, the property or the amount makes the mortgage invalid ([[zzd:170]]).
- A mortgage may secure one's own debt or another person's debt ([[zzd:149]]). A child's property can secure another's debt only with court permission in exceptional cases ([[sk:130:4]]).
- Priority follows the order of entry ([[zzd:169]]). The entry is effective for 10 years and can be renewed ([[zzd:172]]).
- An agreement that the creditor becomes the owner if the debt is not paid, or any other agreed way of satisfaction different from the law, is **invalid** ([[zzd:152]]).
- Material interest: the secured amount ([[znnd:97:1]]).

### The statutory mortgage

The law gives a mortgage to the transferor of real estate over the property, for the claims under the contract (typically **an unpaid price**), and to a co-sharer who is owed compensation after a partition. It is entered at the creditor's request with a copy of the deed ([[zzd:168]]).

### Transfer of a secured claim

A transfer, pledge or attachment of a claim secured by a mortgage needs written form with notarially certified signatures and **entry** in the property register to be effective ([[zzd:171]]). This is common when banks sell loan portfolios.

### Deleting a mortgage

The entry of a mortgage is deleted on the creditor's **consent in notarially certified form** or on a final court decision, by a request with the consent attached ([[zzd:179]]). The fee for certifying the creditor's consent is half of the item 10 fee, at least 25 leva ([[tarifa:12]]). The registry office charges half of the entry fee for a deletion.

> [!example] A sale with a bank loan
> One morning, three acts: (1) the seller's bank certifies its consent to delete the seller's old mortgage, paid from the price; (2) the sale deed; (3) the buyer's mortgage deed in favour of the buyer's bank. The clerk takes the deletion, the sale and the new mortgage to the registry office the same day, in that order. The buyer's bank pays the seller after the entry.
