## The official archive

The official archive of the notary contains the **notarial registers and books**, the **notarial case files**, **other documents** and the **seal** ([[znnd:28:2]], [[n32:1]]). The archive is **inviolable**: nobody has access without the notary's consent, except in the cases of law. A person who gets access keeps the official secret and informs the notary about the acts performed ([[znnd:21]]). Files leave the office only on a written order of a judge or a prosecutor; originals leave only with the notary personally, and an expert examination takes place only in the notary's presence ([[znnd:28:3]], [[znnd:28:4]]).

## The yearly cycle

| When | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | New registers start; numbering of acts, volumes and case files restarts | [[n32:5:1]], [[n32:6:1]], [[n32:10:4]] |
| Every act | Entry in the general register in time order; deeds and wills also in the alphabetical index | [[n32:7]], [[n32:8]] |
| Every day | Entries closed with the notary's signature and seal before the next day's first entry | [[n32:5:1]] |
| Every 200 documents | A volume of the book is closed: numbered, laced, sealed, signed | [[n32:6:1]], [[n32:6:3]] |
| End of the year | The last volumes are closed; the registers end | [[n32:6]] |

Printed registers: each sheet is dated, signed and sealed and attached to the previous one, with signature and seal in at least two places ([[n32:5:5]]). No blank spaces, erasures or unauthorised corrections ([[n32:5:2]], [[n32:5:3]]).

> [!warn] Never destroy without the procedure
> Expired records are destroyed **only after agreement with the State Archives**, with a protocol. A box of old files in a skip outside the office is a breach of secrecy and of data protection law.

## When a notary stops working

When a notary loses the capacity, the archive is **sealed** by order of the president of the district court ([[znnd:36:2]], [[n32:22:1]]). The notary or the heirs ask for the sealing **at once**; in their absence, the mayor, the district judge, the guardian or the curator does, and the president of the court may also order it ex officio ([[n32:22:2]], [[n32:22:4]]). On unsealing, a representative of the Notary Chamber makes an inventory and the archive goes to another notary of the same district who wants it, or to the registration judge; if the heirs name no notary, the Council of Notaries decides who receives it ([[n32:23:2]], [[n32:23:3]]). The seals are destroyed by a commission of the Chamber ([[n32:24]]). The receiving notary performs acts only on the documents in the archive, for example issues copies ([[znnd:38]]).

## Inspections

The Minister of Justice controls every notary through joint inspections by inspectors of the Ministry and inspector-notaries ([[znnd:80б:1]]). An inspection starts on the Minister's initiative, after a complaint, or at the proposal of the Council of Notaries; a protocol records the findings ([[znnd:80б:2]], [[znnd:80б:3]]). The Notary Chamber also supervises notaries ([[znnd:65:1]]).

Inspectors usually check:

- the continuity of the registers, the daily closing and the absence of corrections;
- the volumes of the books and the case files: contents, numbering, signatures;
- the deadlines: entry on the same day, wills to the registration judge within 3 days, notices to the municipality within 7 days;
- the entries in the information system of the Chamber;
- the fees and the bills;
- the anti-money laundering files and internal rules;
- the special bank account and its agreements.

> [!task] Prepare for an inspection
> Pick a random week of last year. For each act in the general register, find the document in the book or the case file, the bill and, for deeds, the proof of entry. Note every gap.

## Professional secrecy

The notary keeps secret all facts learned through the work, and may not use them for any benefit, also after leaving the profession ([[znnd:26]]). Employees keep the same secret ([[znnd:49]]). Information from case files goes only to the parties, their successors and representatives ([[znnd:28а:4]]).

## Data protection

### The office as controller

A notary office processes large amounts of personal data: identity data, identity numbers, addresses, marital status, property, bank accounts, health information in some cases. The notary is the **controller** of these data under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the Personal Data Protection Act. The main legal bases are **legal obligations** (the Notaries Act, the Civil Procedure Code, the AML Act, the tax laws) and the **exercise of official authority** assigned to the notary.

### Copies of identity documents

A controller may copy an identity document, a driving licence or a residence document **only if a law provides for it** ([[zzld:25г]]). The AML Act provides for it in customer due diligence ([[zmip:53:1]]). Outside such cases, the office **records the data** from the document instead of copying it.

### Identity numbers

Free public access to information that contains an **identity number** (ЕГН) or a foreigner's personal number is not allowed, unless a law provides otherwise ([[zzld:25ж]]). Never put identity numbers in e-mail subjects, public lists or messages to third persons.

### Deceased persons

Data of deceased persons may be processed only on a legal ground, with measures that protect the rights of others ([[zzld:25е]]). This matters for wills, heirs and old files.

### Practical security

- **Clean desk**: no documents left at reception; screens locked when the clerk leaves.
- **Access control**: the archive and the safe are locked; keys and codes are personal.
- **Accounts**: personal accounts for the information systems; never share the notary's credentials (the access to the population and identity registers is personal and audited, [[nnbd:10]]).
- **E-mail**: send documents with personal data only to verified addresses, preferably encrypted.
- **Disposal**: shred paper; wipe or destroy disks.
- **Breaches**: a lost laptop, a mis-sent e-mail or a break-in must be reported to the notary at once; under Art. 33 of the GDPR, the controller notifies the Commission for Personal Data Protection within 72 hours when the breach is a risk to people.
- **Video surveillance** at the office needs rules and notices ([[zzld:25д]]).
