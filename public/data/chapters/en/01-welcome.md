## What a notary is

A notary ({{нотариус}}) is a person to whom the state assigns the notarial acts that the laws provide for ([[znnd:2:1]]). The notary is not a civil servant. The notary works in a private office, pays the costs of the office, and lives from the fees of the clients. But each certification that the notary makes has the authority of the state: a court and any institution must accept it as proof, unless somebody proves that it is false ([[gpk:179]]).

Two short facts explain the whole profession:

- **Only a person in the register of the Notary Chamber is a notary** ([[znnd:2:2]]). After a competition, the Minister of Justice orders the entry of the new notary ([[znnd:12:3]]), and the Council of Notaries enters the notary in the register after the oath ([[znnd:14:1]], [[znnd:15]]).
- **The notary must do the notarial work personally** ([[znnd:2:3]]). Staff prepare, check, call, copy, file and deliver. The legal act itself, for example the certification of a signature, is the act of the notary or of an authorised assistant.

> [!note] Why the state uses notaries
> Many transactions are too important to leave to private paper. A sale of an apartment, a mortgage or a power of attorney to sell land can ruin a family if it is false or unclear. The law therefore requires a special form for these transactions. A neutral lawyer checks the identity, the capacity and the will of the parties, explains the consequences and keeps a copy for at least 100 years ([[znnd:28а:3]]).

## What comes through the door

Every office does the same main types of work. The list follows the notarial proceedings in the Civil Procedure Code ([[gpk:569]]).

| Work | Typical example | Who does most of the preparation |
|---|---|---|
| Certification of signatures ({{заверка на подпис}}) | Power of attorney, declaration, consent for a child to travel | Clerk prepares, notary or assistant certifies |
| Certification of signature and content | Power of attorney to sell real estate, transfer of company shares | Clerk and legal assistant |
| Notarial deeds ({{нотариален акт}}) | Sale, gift or exchange of an apartment, mortgage, building right | Legal assistant drafts, notary issues |
| Vehicle contracts | Sale of a used car | Clerk |
| Certification of copies ({{заверка на препис}}) | Copy of a diploma or a deed | Clerk prepares, notary or assistant certifies |
| Notarial invitations ({{нотариална покана}}) | A bank tells a borrower that the whole loan is due | Clerk serves it at the address |
| Wills | Notarial will, deposit and announcement of a holographic will | Notary personally |
| Declaratory deeds | Proof of ownership by documents or by prescription | Legal assistant and notary |
| Inquiries and certificates | A copy of an old deed from the archive | Clerk |

Most offices spend most of their day on certifications of signatures. The sale of real estate is the most important transaction in money and in risk. This course gives each type of work its own chapter.

## Who works in the office

| Role | Legal basis | What the role may do |
|---|---|---|
| Notary ({{нотариус}}) | [[znnd:2]], [[znnd:8]] | All notarial acts in the notary's district. |
| Assistant notary ({{помощник-нотариус}}) | [[znnd:40]], [[znnd:41]] | All acts of the notary on the notary's instructions, **except** deeds for real rights in real estate, acts that recognise ownership, notarial wills and their revocation, and mortgages ([[znnd:40:1]]). |
| Substitute assistant notary ({{помощник-нотариус по заместване}}) | [[znnd:46]] | All acts, alone, while the notary is absent. Needs 3 years of legal experience and an exam. |
| Employees ({{служители}}) | [[znnd:49]], [[znnd:50]] | No notarial acts. They prepare documents, keep registers, collect fees, deliver notices and papers when the notary assigns it, and keep the official secret. |

Employees have different titles in practice: secretary ({{секретар}}), registry clerk ({{деловодител}}), legal assistant ({{юрист}} or {{правен сътрудник}}), archive clerk ({{архивар}}). The law does not name these jobs. It only says that the notary must train and guide the assistant notary and the employees ([[znnd:34:2]]) and that the employees keep the same secret as the notary ([[znnd:49]]).

> [!warn] A clerk never certifies
> Only the notary, an assistant notary within the limits of [[znnd:40]], or an authorised substitute can sign a certification. A certification by a person without this right is null ([[gpk:576]]). If a client asks you to "just stamp it", the answer is no.

## A working day

The Minister of Justice sets the working hours of notary offices ([[znnd:27]]). Deeds that are entered in the property register are issued only in the office and only in working hours ([[gpk:573:2]]). A typical day looks like this:

1. **Before opening.** The clerk checks the e-mail and the phone messages, prints the drafts for today's deeds and checks that the property register and the information system of the Notary Chamber work.
2. **Morning.** A queue of clients for signatures and copies. Each client gives an identity document; the clerk compares it with the person and the document, prepares the endorsement and enters the act in the register. The notary or the assistant notary checks the document in the national register, signs and seals.
3. **Late morning.** The first deed of the day. The parties come with the documents that the office asked for. The notary checks the identity and capacity, reads the deed aloud and the parties sign ([[gpk:578]], [[gpk:579]]).
4. **Same day.** A clerk takes the deed to the registry office ({{Служба по вписванията}}) or sends it electronically. The notary must present deeds for entry on the day of signing ([[znnd:25:5]]).
5. **Afternoon.** Another clerk goes out to serve notarial invitations at addresses. The legal assistant drafts tomorrow's deeds and orders documents from the cadastre and the municipality.
6. **Closing.** The clerk closes the cash and the bills, files the case papers ({{нотариални дела}}) and locks the archive.

## The language of the office

Every notarial document in Bulgaria is in Bulgarian. When a person does not know Bulgarian and the notary does not know the language of that person, the notary appoints an interpreter ([[gpk:582]]). You therefore need the Bulgarian terms even when you study in English. This course writes key Bulgarian terms like this: {{нотариален акт}}. The glossary gives all of them with an English equivalent and the law behind each term.

## How to use this course

- **Chapters.** Seven volumes, from the basics to full simulations. Each chapter starts with objectives and ends with a test and flashcards. Read the chapters in order the first time.
- **Law citations.** A citation such as [[gpk:578:4]] opens the text of the law in the margin, next to the chapter. The library has the full Bulgarian text of each act. In English mode, key articles also have an unofficial English translation.
- **Law library.** Open it from the menu. You can type a citation, for example "чл. 586 ГПК", and go straight to the article.
- **Tests.** Each chapter test shows the answers and the explanations at the end, not after each question. A chapter counts as done when you mark it as read and score at least 80% in its test. The final exam has 40 random questions from all chapters.
- **Flashcards.** The cards use Leitner boxes. A card that you know moves to a higher box and comes back later. A card that you miss goes back to box 1.
- **Templates, checklists and the fee calculator.** The templates and the checklists are study material: read them next to the chapter that uses them. The fee calculator applies the tariff of chapter 24 to an amount.

> [!law] The law changes
> The law texts in the library come from lex.bg and show the date of retrieval and the last amendment in the source. Before you rely on a provision in a real case, check the official text in the State Gazette ({{Държавен вестник}}). Since 1 January 2026 the currency of Bulgaria is the euro. Amounts in leva in older acts count as euro at 1.95583 leva per euro ([[zvevro:11:2]], [[zvevro:12]]).

> [!reform] A reform is under discussion in 2026
> In August 2026 the Ministry of Justice published for public consultation a draft law against property fraud. It proposes, among other things, the entry of announced wills in the information system of the Notary Chamber, a notice of each announced holographic will on the site of the Chamber with one month for objections, a stricter procedure for ownership by prescription, and stricter rules for powers of attorney for real estate. The draft is not law. This course marks the topics that it would change.
