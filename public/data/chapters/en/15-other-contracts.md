## Why these contracts come to the office

Chapter 8 covers the main contracts. This chapter covers the contracts that the exam topics name next to them and that offices certify every week: a third person who takes over a debt, a guarantee, a pledge of a thing or a claim, a lease and an agricultural lease, and the sale of a whole inheritance. Most of them need only written form, but a certification gives proof, a certain date, priority or the possibility of an entry.

## Taking over a debt

| Way | What it means | Form and effect | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joining** a debt ({{встъпване в дълг}}) | A third person becomes a co-debtor, by agreement with the creditor or with the debtor | Both are **jointly** liable. Once the creditor approved the agreement, it cannot be revoked or changed without the creditor | [[zzd:101]] |
| **Replacing** the debtor ({{заместване в дълг}}) | A third person takes the place of the debtor | Only with the **express consent** of the creditor. The old debtor is released. Securities given by third persons end unless they agree; a pledge and a mortgage given by the old debtor stay | [[zzd:102]] |
| Joining or replacing in a debt **secured by a mortgage** | The same, for a mortgage loan | **Written form with notarially certified signatures** and **entry** in the property register, or the change has no effect | [[zzd:171]] |

A **guarantee** ({{поръчителство}}) binds the guarantor to the creditor of another person for the performance of that person's obligation. It must be **in writing** ([[zzd:138]]). The law does not require a certification, but banks often ask for one, because a certified signature cannot be denied later.

> [!example] A buyer takes over the seller's mortgage
> Ivan sells a flat with a mortgage of Bank A. Georgi, the buyer, agrees with the bank to replace Ivan as the debtor. The bank's express consent, Ivan's release and Georgi's obligation are written in one contract; the signatures are certified and the contract is entered in the partida of the flat ([[zzd:102]], [[zzd:171]]). Without the entry, the change has no effect for the mortgage.

## Pledge

### Pledge of a movable thing

- The pledge contract is **valid only if the thing is handed** to the creditor, or to a person chosen by the creditor and the pledgor ([[zzd:156]]).
- When the secured claim is above the small amount of the act, the pledge can be relied upon against third persons only with a **written document with a certain date** that names the things and the claim ([[zzd:156]]). A notarial certification of the date gives that certain date (chapter 18).
- The creditor keeps the thing until the claim is paid in full and may not use it, unless agreed otherwise ([[zzd:157]]).
- The creditor has priority from the price of the thing only if the creditor did not return it to the debtor ([[zzd:159]]).
- For a money claim and a written pledge contract, the creditor may ask the court for an **order for immediate enforcement** on the basis of the contract ([[zzd:160]], [[gpk:418]]).

### Pledge of a claim

A claim that can be transferred can be pledged. The pledge can be relied upon against third persons only if the **debtor was notified**, and, above the small amount, with a document with a certain date ([[zzd:162]]). The pledgor hands over the documents that prove the claim ([[zzd:163]]); the creditor collects the interest and the principal and keeps them as security ([[zzd:164]]).

The pledge of company shares, receivables or a whole enterprise without handing them over is a **special pledge** under its own act (chapter 14). The rules of the Obligations and Contracts Act do not replace the special rules of other laws ([[zzd:161]]).

## Lease

- A **lease** ({{наем}}) obliges the landlord to give a thing for temporary use and the tenant to pay a price ([[zzd:228]]).
- A lease may not be concluded for more than **ten years**, unless it is a commercial deal. Persons who may perform only acts of ordinary management cannot conclude a lease for more than **three years**. A longer term is reduced to ten or three years ([[zzd:229]]).
- If the tenant keeps using the thing after the term with the knowledge and without the objection of the landlord, the lease continues for an indefinite term ([[zzd:236]]).
- When the property is sold, an **entered** lease binds the buyer; a lease with a **certain date** binds the buyer for at most one year from the transfer ([[zzd:237]]). Leases of real estate for more than one year are entered ([[pv:4]]), and an act for entry needs at least a notarially certified signature ([[pv:3:1]]).

## Agricultural lease

The agricultural lease ({{аренда}}) gives agricultural land, or the things for agricultural production, for temporary use against a lease payment ([[zaz:2:1]]). The notary has a special role in it.

| Rule | Article |
|---|---|
| Written form with the signatures of the parties certified **at the same time**; the same for an amendment or a termination | [[zaz:3:1]] |
| The notary **checks** that the lessor owns the land, or more than **50%** of a co-owned plot, alone or with co-owners who gave an express notarially certified power | [[zaz:3:1]], [[zaz:3:4]] |
| The notary keeps **sketches** of the plots, obtained by remote access to the cadastre or from a party; where there is no cadastral map, sketches of the municipal agricultural office | [[zaz:3:1]] |
| The contract is **entered** in the registry office and **registered** in the municipal agricultural office, with the sketches | [[zaz:3:2]] |
| The minimum term is **five** business years | [[zaz:4:1]] |
| An extension is written with notarially certified signatures, entered and registered | [[zaz:18:1]] |
| A termination by mutual consent is written with notarially certified signatures; every termination is entered and registered | [[zaz:27:1]], [[zaz:27:2]] |

> [!tip] The fee for an agricultural lease
> For the signatures on agricultural lease contracts and leases of agricultural land, the notarial fee does not depend on the material interest, and it is charged once when the signatures are certified at the same time ([[zspzz:5:3]]). The tariff has no item of its own for these contracts. Ask the notary which item of the ordinary fees the office applies.

## Fees for these contracts

| Contract | Material interest | Fee | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| A contract with a money claim (a loan, a guarantee, a pledge) | The nominal value of the claim | Signatures on a contract with a material interest: 30% of item 8, at least 30 лв. | [[znnd:97:1]], [[tarifa:10]] |
| A lease of real estate | The total rent for the whole term; without a term, for 3 years | Item 10; when the lease is subject to entry, item 9 (50% of item 8, at least 30 лв.) | [[znnd:97:1]], [[tarifa:9]] |
| Sale of an inheritance | The market price of the real estate in it | Item 9, when the contract is subject to entry | [[znnd:97:1]], [[tarifa:9]] |

> [!example] A lease of a shop for five years
> A shop is leased for 5 years at 500 € a month, and the lease is entered. Material interest: 60 × 500 = 30,000 € (= 58,674.90 лв.) ([[znnd:97:1]]). Item 8: 480.50 + 0.5% × 8,674.90 = 523.87 лв. Item 9: 50% = 261.94 лв. = **133.93 €**, plus VAT ([[tarifa:9]]).

## Sale of an inheritance

An heir may sell an inheritance as a whole ({{продажба на наследство}}). Such a sale clearly shows the intention to accept the inheritance ([[zn:49:2]]).

- The seller who sells the whole inheritance without naming its items guarantees only that the seller **is an heir** ([[zzd:212]]).
- The sale must be **written**, with the signatures **notarially certified** ([[zzd:212]]).
- When the inheritance contains real estate, the contract can be relied upon against third persons **only if it is entered** ([[zzd:212]], [[pv:4]], [[zs:112]]).
- The seller hands over what the seller collected or sold from the inheritance before the sale; the buyer reimburses the debts of the inheritance that the seller paid ([[zzd:213]]).
- The material interest is the market price of the real estate in the inheritance ([[znnd:97:1]]).

> [!warn] An inheritance or one item of it?
> The sale of the whole inheritance is different from the sale of one item of the inheritance. A co-heir's sale of a specific item has no effect if the item does not fall to that co-heir in the partition (chapter 13). Ask the client which of the two is meant, and write the contract accordingly.

## What the office checks

| Contract | What the office asks for | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Replacing the debtor of a mortgage loan | The express consent of the bank; certified signatures; entry | [[zzd:102]], [[zzd:171]] |
| Pledge of a movable | That the thing is handed over; a certain date | [[zzd:156]] |
| Pledge of a claim | The notice to the debtor; the documents of the claim | [[zzd:162]], [[zzd:163]] |
| Lease of real estate for more than a year | A certified signature for the entry; a term of at most ten years | [[pv:3:1]], [[zzd:229]] |
| Agricultural lease | More than 50% of the shares or express certified powers; sketches; five years at least | [[zaz:3]], [[zaz:4:1]] |
| Sale of an inheritance | Certified signatures; entry when real estate is in it | [[zzd:212]] |

> [!task] Practice
> Three co-owners hold a field in the shares 30%, 25% and 45%. The owner of 45% wants to lease the whole field to a farmer for four years. (1) Can the 45% owner sign alone? (2) What is wrong with the term? (3) What happens after the signing? Answers: (1) no; the lessor must hold more than 50%, alone or with an express notarially certified power of another co-owner ([[zaz:3:4]]); (2) the minimum term is five business years ([[zaz:4:1]]); (3) the contract is entered and registered in the municipal agricultural office, with the sketches ([[zaz:3:2]]).
