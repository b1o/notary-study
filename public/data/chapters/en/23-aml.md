## Why the notary is involved

Real estate and company deals are classic channels for money laundering: criminal money becomes a flat, a company share or a "clean" sale price. The Measures Against Money Laundering Act ({{ЗМИП}}) therefore lists **notaries and substitute assistant notaries** among the **obliged persons** ([[zmip:4]]). The obligations apply to the whole office: the clerk who takes the documents, the legal assistant who drafts the deed, and the notary who signs.

The measures are ([[zmip:3]]):

1. **customer due diligence** ({{комплексна проверка}});
2. collecting and preparing documents and information;
3. **keeping** the documents and information;
4. **risk assessment**;
5. **reporting** suspicious operations, deals and clients;
6. reporting other information under the act.

## When due diligence is required

The office applies customer due diligence ([[zmip:11:1]]):

| Situation | Threshold |
|---|---|
| A business relationship is established | Always |
| An **occasional deal** (one deal, or several related deals) | **15,000 euro or more** |
| An occasional deal paid **in cash** | **5,000 euro or more** |
| Any **suspicion** of money laundering, terrorist financing or criminal funds | **Regardless of the amount** |
| A **doubt** about the truth or adequacy of the identification data collected | Regardless of the amount |

Most real estate sales exceed 15,000 euro, so due diligence is the normal case.

## What due diligence includes

Customer due diligence includes ([[zmip:10]]):

- **identifying** the client and verifying the identity from reliable and independent sources;
- identifying the **beneficial owner** and verifying it, including the structure of ownership and control of a legal entity;
- collecting information about the **purpose and nature** of the relationship or deal;
- clarifying the **origin of the funds** where required;
- ongoing monitoring of the relationship.

### Natural persons

Identification uses an **official identity document**, and the office **takes a copy** of it ([[zmip:53:1]]). The office collects ([[zmip:53:2]]):

- the names;
- the date and place of birth;
- the personal identification number, from a **valid** identity document **with a photo**;
- every **citizenship** the person holds;
- the country of permanent residence and the address (a post office box is not enough).

### Legal entities

A legal entity is identified with an original or a notarially certified copy of the official extract of its **current status** and a certified copy of its founding document; for entities in public registers, through a check in the register ([[zmip:54]]).

### Beneficial owners

The **beneficial owner** ({{действителен собственик}}) is the natural person who ultimately owns or controls the client. The office identifies every beneficial owner of a client that is a legal entity ([[zmip:59]]):

1. a check in the register where the beneficial owners are entered (Commercial Register, BULSTAT) ([[zmip:63]]);
2. documents that show the ownership and control;
3. a **declaration** by the legal representative or agent, when the first two are insufficient or contradictory ([[zmip:59:1]]).

### Acting for somebody else

The office establishes whether the client acts **in its own name and for its own account**. If an agent acts, the office asks for proof of the power and identifies both the agent and the principal. If the client seems to act for a hidden third person, this is a ground for a report ([[zmip:65]]).

## Origin of the funds

The **origin of funds** ({{произход на средствата}}) is clarified by at least **two** of the methods of the act: information from the client about the main activity, information from official independent sources such as public registers, information collected under other laws, and others. If the methods fail or contradict each other, and in occasional deals where two methods are impossible, the client gives a **written declaration** on the form of the implementing regulations ([[zmip:66]]).

> [!example] In a real estate sale
> The buyer pays 180,000 euro. The office asks how the money was obtained: a mortgage loan (bank documents), the sale of another flat (that deed), savings (bank statements), an inheritance. The buyer signs the declaration of origin of funds. A buyer who refuses to explain, or whose story does not fit, is a reason to stop and inform the notary.

## Politically exposed persons

A **politically exposed person** ({{видна политическа личност}}) holds or held an important public function in Bulgaria, another Member State, a third country or an international organisation ([[zmip:36]]): heads of state and government, ministers and deputy ministers, members of parliament, members of constitutional and supreme courts, members of the court of auditors and central bank boards, ambassadors, senior officers, members of the boards of state-owned companies, **mayors and deputy mayors, chairs of municipal councils**, members of party governing bodies, and leaders of international organisations. Their family members and close associates are also covered ([[zmip:36:1]]).

For such persons the office applies **enhanced due diligence** ([[zmip:36:1]]). The office must have an internal system to find out whether a client is a politically exposed person; the system may use a written declaration, but **not only** the declaration ([[zmip:42:3]]).

Clients from countries that do not apply international standards against money laundering also need enhanced measures ([[zmip:46]]).

## Keeping records

The office keeps all documents, data and information collected under the act for **5 years** ([[zmip:67:1]]). For occasional deals, the period runs from the date of the deal ([[zmip:67:3]]). The records must allow quick retrieval as evidence in court ([[zmip:69]]).

## Reporting a suspicion

When the office **suspects** or **knows** that money is laundered or that funds have a criminal origin, it must **notify the Financial Intelligence Directorate of the State Agency for National Security** ({{дирекция „Финансово разузнаване“ на ДАНС}}) **at once, before the deal**, and **delay** the deal within the permitted time, stating in the notice how long the deal can be postponed ([[zmip:72:1]]). If the delay is objectively impossible, the office notifies immediately after the deal and explains why ([[zmip:72:2]]). The director of the directorate may suspend a deal for up to **5 working days** from the day after the order; if no distraint or injunction follows in that time, the deal may go ahead ([[zmip:73:1]]).

An employee may report even if not responsible for the AML measures; the directorate keeps the employee anonymous ([[zmip:72:3]]).

> [!warn] No tipping off
> The office, its management and its employees **must not tell the client or third persons** that information was disclosed to the directorate ([[zmip:80]]). Never say "we reported you" or hint at a report. If the deal is delayed, the notary gives a neutral reason.

### Warning signs in a notary office

- The buyer pays far above or below the market price, or the declared price changes at the last minute.
- The money comes from third persons with no visible link to the buyer, or from high-risk countries.
- Complex company chains with no economic reason, nominee directors, very recent companies.
- Repeated purchases and resales of the same property within a short time.
- A client who is in a hurry, avoids questions, or asks the office to split payments to stay under thresholds.
- Agents with general powers acting for persons who never appear.
- Cash: payments of 10,000 leva (5,112.92 euro) or more in cash are prohibited anyway ([[zopvb:3:1]]).

## Organisation in the office

- **Risk assessment.** The notary prepares an own assessment of the risks, taking into account clients, countries, services, deals and channels ([[zmip:98]]).
- **Internal rules.** The notary adopts internal rules with criteria for recognising suspicious clients and deals, internal control, the system for politically exposed persons, the client risk profiles, training and other topics ([[zmip:101]]).
- **Internal control.** The notary performs the internal control personally or designates a senior employee in writing ([[zmip:107]]).
- **Training.** Every employee knows the internal rules and the warning signs, and knows to whom inside the office to report a concern.
