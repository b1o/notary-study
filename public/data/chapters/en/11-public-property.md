## Why the office meets public property

Many titles in Bulgaria pass through the state or a municipality: land returned after 1991, flats and plots sold by municipalities, plots used for streets, houses on municipal land. When such a property comes to the office, three questions decide the file: was it **public** or **private** property of the state or the municipality, how did it leave them, and can somebody have acquired it by **prescription**?

## Kinds of ownership

Ownership belongs to the state, the municipalities, cooperatives and other legal entities, and citizens; all kinds of ownership have the same protection ([[zs:2]]). The property of the state and of the municipalities is **public** or **private** ([[zs:6]]). Separate laws set their regime ([[zs:7]]): the State Property Act ({{Закон за държавната собственост}}, ЗДС) and the Municipal Property Act ({{Закон за общинската собственост}}, ЗОС).

| | State | Municipality |
|---|---|---|
| Public property | Objects of exclusive state property, property declared public by law or by the Council of Ministers, property given to state bodies for their functions, property of national importance for public use ([[zds:2:2]]) | Property declared public by law, property for the functions of the local authorities, other property for lasting local public needs set by the municipal council ([[zos:3:2]]) |
| Private property | All other state property ([[zds:2:3]]) | All other municipal property ([[zos:3:3]]) |
| Who changes public into private | The Council of Ministers ([[zds:6:1]]) | The municipal council, by two thirds of all councillors ([[zos:6:1]], [[zos:6:3]]) |

> [!warn] A company owned by the state is not the state
> The property of commercial companies and non-profit entities is **not** state or municipal property, even if the state or the municipality owned all the property transferred into them ([[zds:2:4]], [[zos:2:2]]). A sale by a municipal EAD follows the rules for companies (chapter 14), not the rules of this chapter.

## What may and may not happen with public property

- Public state property **cannot be disposed of** and **cannot be acquired by prescription** ([[zds:7:1]]). Private state property can be disposed of and acquired by prescription, under the Property Act ([[zds:7:3]]).
- Public municipal property, the land of the municipal land fund and municipal forest territories **cannot be acquired by prescription** ([[zos:7:1]]). Public municipal property cannot be transferred to third persons, except to the state under a special law ([[zos:7:2]]). Private municipal property can be disposed of under the general rules ([[zos:7:3]]).
- Public state or municipal property is not restored to former owners ([[zds:7:4]], [[zos:7:4]]).
- The Property Act says the same for every public thing: it cannot be acquired by prescription ([[zs:86]]).

> [!law] The moratorium on prescription, 2006–2022
> From 1 June 2006, prescription for the acquisition of **private** state and municipal property **stopped running**. The rule is § 1 of the transitional provisions of the act amending the Property Act (ДВ, бр. 46 от 2006 г.), which the library does not contain. Parliament extended the term several times. The Constitutional Court declared the last extension unconstitutional (Decision No. 3 of 2022, ДВ, бр. 18 от 2022 г.). Prescription runs again from 8 March 2022, the day the decision took effect, and the years of the moratorium do not count (news.lex.bg, 8 March 2022). Before a circumstantial verification for a property that was state or municipal, count only the possession before 1 June 2006 and after 7 March 2022.

## Acts of state and municipal property

- The state and the municipalities certify the creation, change and end of their ownership with an **act of state property** or an **act of municipal property** ({{акт за държавна собственост}}, {{акт за общинска собственост}}) ([[zds:5:1]], [[zos:5:1]]).
- The act is an **official document** made by an official in the form of the law ([[zds:5:2]], [[zos:5:2]]). It **does not create** the right ([[zds:5:3]], [[zos:5:3]]): the right comes from the ground stated in the act, such as a law, a deal or an expropriation.
- An act of state property is made in two copies by the "State Property" unit of the regional governor and presented for entry within 14 days ([[zds:68:1]], [[zds:70:1]]). An act of municipal property is made in two copies, approved by the mayor and presented for entry within 7 days ([[zos:56:1]], [[zos:58:1]]).
- Acts of state and municipal property are entered in the property register ([[pv:4]]).

> [!tip] Reading an act in a chain of title
> Check the date of the act, its number, whether it is **public** or **private** property, the **ground** stated in it, and the entry. An act of private municipal property is not a sale; the next document in the chain must show how the property left the municipality. An act of public property means that the property could not be sold and could not be acquired by prescription.

## Deals with the state and the municipalities

| | State | Municipality |
|---|---|---|
| Ways to dispose of private property | Sale, exchange, partition, limited real rights, contribution to a company, on independent valuations not lower than the tax assessments ([[zds:43:2]]) | Sale, exchange, gift, partition, limited real rights ([[zos:34:4]]) |
| Who sells | The Agency for Public Enterprises and Control, for a tax assessment above 10,000 лв. (5,112.92 €); the regional governor up to that amount; both on the electronic platform ([[zds:44:1]], [[zds:44:2]]) | The mayor, after a decision of the municipal council, by public auction or public competition ([[zos:35:1]], [[zos:35:6]]) |
| Without an auction | Land under a lawfully built building, to the owner of the building, by the regional governor ([[zds:44:3]]) | Land under a lawfully built building, to the owner of the building; deals with the state or other municipalities ([[zos:35:3]], [[zos:35:4]]) |
| Form | **Written form**, entered by the registration judge of the property's district ([[zds:48:2]]) | **Written form**, signed by the mayor, entered by the registration judge of the property's district ([[zos:34:7]]) |

The contract is **not** a notarial deed. The notary meets these contracts as titles in a later sale, and checks their signatories, the decision behind them and the entry. For a disposal by a municipality, the act of municipal property must be entered by the date of the contract ([[zos:34:8]]). A gift of private municipal property needs a decision of three quarters of all councillors ([[zos:35:5]]). The Spatial Development Act also uses written form when the state or a municipality changes plot boundaries or is a co-owner of a plot (chapter 10; [[zut:15:4]], [[zut:15:5]], [[zut:183:3]]).

## Restitution

After 1991, laws returned property that the state took after 1944. Two acts appear most often in titles.

### Agricultural land

The rights of owners or their heirs to agricultural land that they owned before the land was taken into cooperative or state farms are restored ([[zspzz:10:1]]). The municipal agricultural office decides ([[zspzz:14:1]]):

- **In existing or restorable old boundaries** (item 1): the decision describes the size, the category, the location, the boundaries and the restrictions of the plot, with a **sketch**. The **final decision with the sketch certifies ownership and has the force of a declaratory notarial deed**, except for land inside settlements restored under Art. 10(7) ([[zspzz:10:7]]). For a deceased owner, the decision is issued jointly for the heirs; their shares follow the Inheritance Act and are not stated in the decision.
- **In new boundaries** (item 2): the decision states the size, the categories and the area, and the plot is formed by a plan for land division.

No state fee is paid for the entry of a decision with the force of a declaratory deed ([[zspzz:5:2]]). A declaratory deed for agricultural land or forest costs 20 лв. (10.23 €) ([[tarifa:1]]).

### Urban property

The Restitution Act ({{ЗВСОНИ}}) returns, **by law**, real estate expropriated under the laws that it lists, if on its entry into force the property belonged to the state, a municipality or their companies and existed in its real dimensions ([[zvsoni:1:1]], [[zvsoni:2:1]], [[zvsoni:2:3]]). Ownership returns to the persons from whom it was taken or to their heirs; a dispute between claimants goes to court ([[zvsoni:3:1]]). It is not returned if the owner received compensation in money or in an equivalent property ([[zvsoni:4:1]]).

Prescription that ran for property restored under this act or under the agricultural land act **does not count**; it starts to run again from the day this rule entered into force ([[zvsoni:5:2]]).

> [!note] What the office asks for
> For land returned in old boundaries: the final decision with the sketch, and the certificate of heirs when the decision names the heirs of a deceased owner. For urban property returned by law: the documents of the old ownership and of the expropriation, and the proof that no compensation was received; the notary decides whether they prove ownership ([[gpk:586:2]]). The library holds only part of these acts; the regulations of the agricultural land act and other restitution laws are not imported.

## A checklist for the chain of title

| Document in the chain | What it means | What the office checks |
|---|---|---|
| Act of state or municipal property | The state or municipality recorded its right | Public or private; the ground; the entry ([[zds:5]], [[zos:5]]) |
| Contract with the state or a municipality | A disposal of private property | Written form; the decision or the auction; the signatory; the entry ([[zds:48:2]], [[zos:34:7]], [[zos:35:1]]) |
| Decision of the agricultural office in old boundaries | A title with the force of a declaratory deed, except for land in settlements under Art. 10(7) | Final; with a sketch; heirs and their shares ([[zspzz:14:1]]) |
| Restitution of urban property | Ownership returned by law | Expropriation under a listed law; real existence; no compensation ([[zvsoni:2]], [[zvsoni:4:1]]) |
| Prescription against the state or a municipality | Possible only for private property | Not public property ([[zs:86]]); no years of the moratorium |

> [!task] Practice
> Petar has used a strip of land next to his yard since 1998. He asks for a circumstantial verification. The municipality has an act of private municipal property for the strip from 2004. (1) Can Petar rely on prescription? (2) Which years count? (3) What changes if the act says "public municipal property"? Answers: (1) yes in principle, for private municipal property, if it is not land of the municipal land fund ([[zos:7:1]], [[zos:7:3]]); (2) from 1998 to 31 May 2006 and from 8 March 2022, which is more than 12 years by the end of 2026, if the possession was not interrupted ([[zs:79]], [[zs:81]]); (3) public property cannot be acquired by prescription at all ([[zs:86]], [[zos:7:1]]).
