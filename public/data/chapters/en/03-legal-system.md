## Why a clerk needs the legal system

Each document in a notary office applies a rule from a specific act. A client asks "why do you need my marriage certificate?" and the right answer names the rule. When a law changes, the office must change its forms on the day the law enters into force. This chapter gives you the map.

## The sources of law and their order

| Level | Source | Example from notarial work |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Constitution: the supreme law, with direct effect ([[konst:5:1]], [[konst:5:2]]) | Foreigners may acquire land only under the conditions of the Constitution ([[konst:22]]) |
| 2 | Ratified international treaties that are in force: they are part of domestic law and prevail over conflicting laws ([[konst:5:4]]) | The Hague Apostille Convention for documents from abroad |
| 2a | Law of the European Union: a regulation applies directly and prevails over a conflicting national act ([[zna:15:2]]) | The General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679; the Succession Regulation (EU) No 650/2012 |
| 3 | Laws and codes, adopted by the National Assembly ([[zna:3]], [[zna:4]]) | ЗННД, ГПК, ЗЗД, ЗС, СК |
| 4 | Acts of the Council of Ministers and of ministers: decrees, regulations, ordinances, instructions, tariffs ([[zna:6]], [[zna:7]]) | Regulations on Entries ([[pv:1]]), Ordinance No. 32 on the archives of notaries ([[n32:1]]), the Tariff of notarial fees ([[tarifa:8]]) |
| 5 | Ordinances of the municipal councils, for local matters ([[zna:8]]) | The rate of the local tax on the acquisition of property in each municipality ([[zmdt:1:2]]) |

Two rules follow from the table:

- **A lower act must agree with every higher act** ([[zna:15:1]]). An ordinance can only implement the matter for which the law allows it ([[zna:12]]).
- **Acts do not apply back in time**, except where an express rule says so ([[zna:14:1]]).

> [!law] When does a new rule start to apply?
> All normative acts are published in the State Gazette ({{Държавен вестник}}). They enter into force **three days after promulgation**, unless the act sets another date ([[konst:5:5]]). Many amendments of notarial law set a later date, so read the final provisions of each amending law.

### Case law and practice

Courts decide individual cases, but the Supreme Court of Cassation ({{Върховен касационен съд}}, ВКС) also adopts **interpretative decisions** ({{тълкувателни решения}}). These decisions bind the courts and the administration. Notaries follow them in practice because a court will judge their acts by them. The Notary Chamber also issues guidance to notaries. Guidance is not law, but it shows how the profession applies the law.

## Courts and bodies around the notary

| Body | Why it matters to the office |
|---|---|
| District court ({{районен съд}}) | The district of a notary is the district of a district court ([[znnd:3]]). Each district court has registration judges ({{съдии по вписванията}}) who order entries in the property register ([[zsv:279]], [[zsv:280]]). |
| Regional court ({{окръжен съд}}) | Hears appeals against a refusal of the notary to perform a certification ([[gpk:577:1]]). |
| Court of appeal ({{апелативен съд}}) | The notarial colleges follow the districts of the courts of appeal ([[znnd:4:3]]). |
| Supreme Court of Cassation | Hears appeals in disciplinary cases against notaries ([[znnd:80:1]]); a judge of the court sits in the competition commission ([[znnd:12:2]]). |
| Administrative courts | Hear appeals against orders of the Minister of Justice about entries and transfers of notaries ([[znnd:12:4]], [[znnd:34б:5]]). |

| Notary Chamber ({{Нотариална камара}}) | All notaries are members; it keeps the register, the information system and the disciplinary bodies ([[znnd:4]], [[znnd:52]]). |

## How to read a citation

A Bulgarian article ({{член}}, abbreviated "чл.") can have paragraphs ({{алинея}}, "ал."), items ({{точка}}, "т.") and letters ({{буква}}, "б."). The act comes last, as an abbreviation.

| Bulgarian form | Meaning | In this course (English mode) |
|---|---|---|
| чл. 25, ал. 9 ЗННД | Article 25, paragraph 9 of the Notaries Act | [[znnd:25:9]] |
| чл. 8, ал. 1, т. 3 ЗННД | Article 8, paragraph 1, item 3 | [[znnd:8:1:3]] |
| чл. 40, ал. 1, т. 1 – 4 ЗННД | Items 1 to 4 | [[znnd:40:1|Art. 40(1), items 1–4 ЗННД]] |
| § 2 от ДР на ЗННД | Paragraph 2 of the Additional Provisions | [[znnd:§2]] |
| т. 8 от ТНТ | Item 8 of the Tariff of notarial fees | [[tarifa:8]] |

Other parts of an act:

- **Additional provisions** ({{Допълнителни разпоредби}}, ДР) often define the terms of the act. For example, [[znnd:§1|§ 1 of the Additional Provisions of ЗННД]] defines the notarial fee.
- **Transitional and final provisions** ({{Преходни и заключителни разпоредби}}, ПЗР) settle old cases and the date of entry into force.
- **Amendment notes** in brackets, such as "(Изм. – ДВ, бр. 84 от 2023 г., в сила от 06.10.2023 г.)", say which issue of the State Gazette changed the text and from when. "Нов/нова" means new, "изм." amended, "доп." supplemented, "отм." repealed.

## Where to find the law

- **The State Gazette** (dv.parliament.bg) publishes the official text. Only the State Gazette is official.
- **Consolidated texts.** lex.bg is free. Most offices also pay for a legal information system such as Ciela or Apis, which add case law and commentary.
- **Bills and drafts.** Bills of laws are on parliament.bg. Drafts of ordinances and laws of the Council of Ministers go to public consultation on strategy.bg.
- **Case law.** The website of the Supreme Court of Cassation and the central portal for court acts.
- **The Notary Chamber.** Its website has the register of notaries, guidance and forms.

## Basic legal ideas you will use every day

### Legal capacity and capacity to act

Every person can hold rights from birth: this is legal capacity ({{правоспособност}}) ([[zls:1]]). Capacity to act ({{дееспособност}}) is the ability to acquire rights and obligations by one's own acts:

- **Adults** (18 and over) have full capacity to act ([[zls:2]]).
- **Minors under 14** ({{малолетни}}) cannot act; their parents or guardians act for them ([[zls:3]]).
- **Minors from 14 to 18** ({{непълнолетни}}) act themselves **with the consent** of their parents or curators, except for small everyday deals ([[zls:4]]).
- **Persons under judicial disability** ({{поставени под запрещение}}): full disability makes a person incapable, limited disability is like the position of a minor of 14 to 18 ([[zls:5]]).

Chapter 7 explains how the office checks capacity in practice.

### Transactions, contracts and statements

A legal transaction ({{сделка}}) is a statement of will that creates, changes or ends rights. A **contract** ({{договор}}) needs two or more parties. A **unilateral** statement ({{едностранно волеизявление}}), such as a power of attorney or a waiver, needs one party; the rules on contracts apply to it accordingly ([[zzd:44]]).

### Real rights and obligations

- **Real rights** ({{вещни права}}) are rights over a thing that everybody must respect: ownership, building right, right of use, servitudes, mortgage. The law gives a closed list of them. They usually need a notarial deed and entry in the property register.
- **Obligations** ({{облигационни права}}, {{задължения}}) bind only the parties: a loan, a lease, a preliminary contract.

### Immovable and movable things

Immovable things ({{недвижими вещи}}) are the land, the plants, the buildings and everything that is permanently attached to the land or to a building. All other things, including energy, are movable ([[zs:110]]).

### The form of a transaction

The law sometimes requires a special form. The three forms you will meet most are:

1. **simple written form**, for example a preliminary contract for the sale of real estate ([[zzd:19]]);
2. **written form with notarial certification of the signatures**, for example the sale of a car;
3. **a notarial deed** ({{нотариален акт}}), for every contract that transfers ownership of real estate or creates other real rights in real estate ([[zzd:18]]).

A contract without the form that the law requires is **void** ([[zzd:26:2]]). This is why the notary is not a formality: without the notary, the deal does not exist.

### Void and voidable

| | Void ({{нищожен}}) | Voidable ({{унищожаем}}) |
|---|---|---|
| Grounds | Contrary to law or good morals, impossible object, no consent, no required form, no cause, a sham contract ([[zzd:26]]) | Concluded by a person without capacity, by mistake, fraud, threat or extreme need ([[zzd:27]]) |
| Effect | Never produces effect; anyone can rely on the nullity | Valid until a court annuls it at the request of the protected party ([[zzd:32]]) |
| Time limit | None | Three years for most grounds, counted from the end of the disability or the discovery of the mistake or fraud ([[zzd:32]]); one year for extreme need ([[zzd:33:2]]) |
| Can it be confirmed? | No | Yes, by a written act or by voluntary performance, except a contract concluded in extreme need ([[zzd:35]]) |

After nullity or annulment, each party returns what it received ([[zzd:34]]).

### Official and private documents

- An **official document** ({{официален документ}}) is issued by an official within the official's competence and in the prescribed form. It proves the statements made before the official and the acts done by the official ([[gpk:179:1]]). A notarial deed and the notary's endorsement on a document are official documents.
- A **private document** ({{частен документ}}) signed by its authors proves only that they made the statements in it ([[gpk:180]]).

This is why a notarial certification of a signature is valuable: the endorsement is an official document that proves who signed and when.

### Time limits

Time limits run in years, months, weeks and days ([[gpk:60:1]]). The last day lasts until midnight; if something must be filed with the court, the term ends when the working hours end ([[gpk:62:1]]). Notarial law has many short limits: entry on the same day ([[znnd:25:5]]), seven days for changes in the register ([[znnd:16:2]]), and others.
