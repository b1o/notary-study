## The legal frame

Notarial fees are set by a **tariff** adopted by the Council of Ministers on a proposal of the Minister of Justice after agreement with the Notary Chamber ([[znnd:85:3]]). The same act costs the same, whether a notary or a state body performs it ([[znnd:85:2]]). The fee is the payment for the notary's service ([[znnd:§1]]). The tariff in force dates from 1998, last amended in 2009; its amounts are in leva and count as euro at 1.95583 since 1 January 2026 ([[zvevro:11:2]], [[zvevro:12]], [[zvevro:13]]).

## Four kinds of fees

| Kind | What it depends on | Examples | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary** ({{обикновени}}) | Fixed amounts for acts listed in the tariff | Signatures without material interest, copies, invitations, wills deposits | [[znnd:90]], [[tarifa:1]]–[[tarifa:7]] |
| **Proportional** ({{пропорционални}}) | The certified **material interest** or the time spent; with a minimum and a maximum | Notarial deeds, contracts with a value, wills | [[znnd:91]], [[znnd:92]], [[tarifa:8]]–[[tarifa:13]] |
| **By time** | Hours spent; only for notaries | Consultations, mediation, obtaining documents | [[znnd:93]], [[tarifa:14]]–[[tarifa:16]] |
| **For drafting and checking** | The fee of the act; half for a check | Draft deed, check of a client's draft | [[znnd:94]], [[tarifa:17]]–[[tarifa:22]] |

Plus an **additional fee** for acts outside the office, out of hours or on holidays ([[znnd:95]], [[tarifa:23]]).

### Rules worth remembering

- **No fee for an oral consultation** given in connection with another act that is charged ([[znnd:93:2]]).
- A **draft** costs the same as the certification; a **check** of a draft costs half. If the same notary certifies the document within one month, only the other half is charged ([[znnd:94]], [[tarifa:22]]). Drafts of the acts of item 1 cost the item 1 fee ([[tarifa:17]]); a draft deed costs item 8, and a draft will or a draft contract with a value costs item 11, a draft will only when the will is not made on the same day ([[tarifa:20]]).
- Time-based fees per hour: 8 лв. (4.09 €) for an oral consultation, 15 лв. (7.67 €) for a written consultation and for mediation, 5 лв. (2.56 €) for inquiries and obtaining documents ([[tarifa:14]], [[tarifa:15]], [[tarifa:16]]).
- For time-based fees, a started hour counts as a full hour; the notary states the expected time in advance ([[tarifa:16]]).
- When the Commerce Act or the Special Pledges Act requires the content to be certified together with the signatures, an **ordinary** fee applies ([[znnd:90:2]]).

## The material interest

The **material interest** ({{материален интерес}}) is the value that the act certifies. The law fixes it for most acts ([[znnd:96]], [[znnd:97]]):

| Act | Material interest |
|---|---|
| Transfer or certification of ownership of a thing | The **market price** of the thing |
| Exchange | The market price of the **more valuable** thing |
| Voluntary partition | The market price of **all the shares** |
| Building right | **90%** of the market price of the land (or the part concerned) |
| Right of use | The market **rent** for the whole term, or for **3 years** if no term |
| Motor vehicle | The value for which it is **insured** |
| Commercial enterprise | The **book value** of the fixed assets for the last quarter |
| Founding a company | The **capital** |
| Sale of an inheritance | The market price of the real estate in it |
| Money claims (loans, mortgages, guarantees) | The **nominal value**; periodic payments: the total for the term, or for 3 years |
| Changes and additions to a deal | Only the value of the **changes** |

Two corrections apply:

- If the market price and the **tax assessment** differ, the fee is charged on the **higher** ([[znnd:96:3]]). The market price is assessed under the Local Taxes Act ([[znnd:§2]]).
- In onerous deals, the **contract price** applies if it is higher than the value under these rules ([[znnd:97:3]]).

## Item 8: the proportional scale

| Material interest (leva) | Fee (leva) | In euro |
|---|---|---|
| up to 100 | 30 | up to 51.13 € → 15.34 € |
| 101 – 1,000 | 30 + 1.5% over 100 | 15.34 € + 1.5% over 51.13 € |
| 1,001 – 10,000 | 43.50 + 1.3% over 1,000 | 22.24 € + 1.3% over 511.29 € |
| 10,001 – 50,000 | 160.50 + 0.8% over 10,000 | 82.06 € + 0.8% over 5,112.92 € |
| 50,001 – 100,000 | 480.50 + 0.5% over 50,000 | 245.68 € + 0.5% over 25,564.59 € |
| 100,001 – 500,000 | 730.50 + 0.2% over 100,000 | 373.50 € + 0.2% over 51,129.19 € |
| over 500,000 | 1,530.50 + 0.1% over 500,000, **max 6,000** | 782.53 € + 0.1% over 255,645.94 €, **max 3,067.75 €** |

Source: [[tarifa:8]]. Other proportional items are fractions of item 8:

- **Item 9**: 50%, at least 30 лв. (signatures on contracts subject to entry, notarial wills, protests, content with a value, copies of announced wills) ([[tarifa:9]]).
- **Item 10**: 30%, at least 30 лв. (signatures on contracts with a value) ([[tarifa:10]]).
- **Item 11**: 100% (circumstantial verification; vehicle contracts) ([[tarifa:11]]).
- **Item 12**: half of item 10, at least 25 лв. (unilateral statements with a value: receipts, consents to delete mortgages, guarantees) ([[tarifa:12]]).

> [!example] Three calculations
> 1. **Mortgage** securing 100,000 € (= 195,583 лв.): 730.50 + 0.2% × 95,583 = 921.67 лв. = **471.24 €**, plus VAT.
> 2. **Preliminary contract** for 60,000 € (= 117,349.80 лв.), signatures certified: item 8 = 730.50 + 0.2% × 17,349.80 = 765.20 лв.; item 10 = 30% = 229.56 лв. = **117.37 €**.
> 3. **Consent to delete a mortgage** of 40,000 € (= 78,233.20 лв.): item 8 = 480.50 + 0.5% × 28,233.20 = 621.67 лв.; item 10 = 186.50 лв.; item 12 = half = 93.25 лв. = **47.68 €**.

## The additional fee

For an act performed at the request of the client outside the office, out of hours or on holidays, the tariff adds ([[tarifa:23]]):

| Situation | Addition |
|---|---|
| Outside the office, in working hours | 25% |
| In the office, out of hours | 25% |
| Outside the office, out of hours | 50% |
| Outside the office and outside the town | 50% |
| Outside the office, out of hours and outside the town | Double |

The Notaries Act itself speaks of a total of one half of the fee ([[znnd:95:2]]). Follow the practice of your office.

## Who owes the fee, when, and the bill

- The fee is owed by the person whose request the notary considered; several persons are **jointly** liable ([[znnd:87]]).
- It is due **when the act is performed**; for a circumstantial verification, **when the request is filed**. The notary may ask for an **advance** ([[znnd:88]]).
- The office issues a **bill** ({{сметка}}) in two or more copies signed by the notary. It states the provisions, the material interest or the time, the amounts, the additional costs, the advance and the consequences of non-payment ([[znnd:89:1]], [[znnd:89:2]]), on the approved form ([[n32:13:2]]).
- For unpaid fees, the notary may ask for an **order for payment** regardless of the amount ([[znnd:89:3]]).
- If the material interest was wrong, the correct fee is charged on a bill made within **7 days** of discovering the error ([[znnd:89:4]]).
- Between the parties of a sale of real estate, the costs are shared **equally** unless agreed otherwise ([[zzd:186]]).

## VAT

Notarial services are subject to VAT. A notary registered under the VAT Act adds **20%** to the notarial fee. VAT does **not** apply to the entry fee or to the local tax, which are public charges.

## Fees of the Registry Agency

The registry office charges state fees under the tariff of the Registry Agency, collected through the notary for deeds. That tariff is not in the law library; check the current amounts on the site of the Agency:

- **entry** of an act: **0.1%** of the price on which the act was charged, at least **10 лв. (5.11 €)**;
- **notes and deletions**, for example the deletion of a mortgage: **half** of the entry fee;
- **certificates** of encumbrances: 10 лв. in 7 working days, 20 лв. in 3 days, 50 лв. in 8 working hours (5.11 €, 10.23 €, 25.56 €);
- remote **inquiries**: 1 лв. (0.51 €).

## The local tax on acquisition

| Acquisition | Rate (set by the municipal council) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| For value: sale, exchange, care contract, building right | 0.1% – 3% | [[zmdt:47:2]] |
| Prescription (declaratory deed after a circumstantial verification) | The tax on gratuitous acquisition at the gift rate for other persons, 3.3% – 6.6%; the base is the assessment at the time of the deed | [[zmdt:44:2]], [[zmdt:44:3]], [[zmdt:46:1]], [[zmdt:47:1]] |
| Gift between relatives in the direct line and spouses | Exempt | [[zmdt:44:6]] |
| Gift between siblings and their children | 0.4% – 0.8% | [[zmdt:47:1]] |
| Gift to other persons | 3.3% – 6.6% | [[zmdt:47:1]] |
| Partition | On the increase of a share only | [[zmdt:47:3]] |

- The **base** is the agreed price, or the tax assessment if it is higher; a gift has no price, so its base is the tax assessment ([[zmdt:46:2]]).
- The **acquirer** pays, unless agreed otherwise; in an exchange, the person who acquires the more valuable property ([[zmdt:45:1]]).
- The tax is paid in the municipality where the property is ([[zmdt:49:1]]), at the time of the transfer ([[zmdt:49:2]]); the notary establishes that it is paid ([[zmdt:50:1]]).
- Many institutions are exempt, for example the state and municipalities as acquirers ([[zmdt:48]]).

## Cash, accounts and the euro

- Payments of **10,000 лв. (5,112.92 €) or more** are made only by bank transfer or deposit; related payments add up ([[zopvb:3:1]]).
- In onerous real estate deals, payments over 10,000 лв. go through the notary's **special account** or a bank account in a bank chosen by the parties ([[znnd:25:10]], [[znnd:25а]]).
- Amounts in leva in any legal act are converted at **1.95583** and rounded to the cent ([[zvevro:12]], [[zvevro:13]]).
