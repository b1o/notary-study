## The premises

A notary office ({{нотариална кантора}}) has **two or more functionally connected rooms**. The rooms must hold the chancery ({{канцелария}}), where the notary receives the parties, and the official archive ([[znnd:13:2]], [[n32:25:1]], [[n32:25:3]]). The office must keep the archive safe and allow the notary to work properly ([[znnd:13:3]]).

The office must have an outside sign with the words "Нотариална кантора" or "Нотариус" ([[n32:25:2]]). Each notary who works in the office has an individual sign with:

- the words "Нотариална кантора" or "Нотариус";
- the first name and family name of the notary, as in the register of the Chamber;
- the number of the notary in the register ([[n32:26:1]]).

The sign may also show the district, the address and the working hours. It may **not** be used for commercial advertising ([[n32:26:2]], [[n32:26:3]]).

## Working hours

- The Minister of Justice sets the working hours and the cases of temporary closure ([[znnd:27]]).
- The office works in the **same hours as the district court** ([[n32:27:1]]).
- Work on deeds that must be entered ends **at least two hours** before the end of the reception time of the registry office ([[n32:27:2]]). The reason: the deed must reach the registry office the same day ([[znnd:25:5]]).
- During the working hours the office may close temporarily for up to **25 working days a year** in total and up to **2 hours in one day** ([[n32:28]]).

Other notarial acts may be performed outside the office and out of hours when good reasons prevent the parties from coming, or the act is urgent ([[gpk:573:3]]). Deeds that must be entered are issued only in the office and only in working hours ([[gpk:573:2]]).

## The registers and books

Every notary keeps the following registers and books ([[znnd:28а:1]], [[n32:4:1]]):

| No. | Register or book | What goes in | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General register ({{общ регистър}}) | Every act in time order: case files opened, deeds, certifications of content, date, signatures and copies, invitations, protests, protocols, inquiries ([[n32:7]]) | 10 years |
| 2 | Alphabetical index ({{азбучен указател}}) | The names and identity numbers of the parties to deeds, wills and deposits, with their role and the numbers in the registers ([[n32:8]]) | 100 years |
| 3 | Register of holographic wills and deposits | Delivery, return and announcement of holographic wills; deposit and return of documents ([[n32:9]]) | 100 years |
| 4 | Book of acts subject to entry | Notarial deeds and other acts and documents that are entered, and marriage contracts | 100 years |
| 5 | Book of notarial wills | Notarial wills and acts revoking wills | 100 years |
| 6 | Book of invitations, protests, protocols and copies | Notarial invitations, protests, declaratory protocols and copies of documents with certified content | 10 years |
| 7 | Expense book ({{разносна книга}}) | The record of service of papers | 10 years |

After 10 years, the registers and books in rows 1, 6 and 7 are destroyed after agreement with the State Archives. After 100 years, the others go to the State Archives for permanent storage ([[znnd:28а:2]], [[znnd:28а:3]]).

### Rules for every register

- Registers **start each calendar year** ([[n32:5:1]]).
- All entries of the day are **closed with the signature and the seal of the notary** before the first entry of the next day ([[n32:5:1]]).
- No blank spaces, erasures, corrections or additions are allowed, except in the prescribed way ([[n32:5:2]]). Blank spaces are crossed out. An error is corrected with a note, signed and sealed by the notary, that says exactly how to read the correction ([[n32:5:3]]).
- A register kept on a computer is printed. Each printed sheet carries the date, the signature and the seal of the notary and is attached to the previous sheet; the two sheets are joined with the signature and the seal of the notary in at least two places ([[n32:5:4]], [[n32:5:5]]).
- There is only **one** general register ([[n32:7:3]]).

### Books in volumes

Books 4, 5 and 6 consist of the documents themselves, in the order in which they were made, in **volumes of 200 documents** ([[n32:6:1]]). The numbering of documents and volumes **starts again each year**. This is why a deed is cited as "акт № 112, том III, рег. № 4567, дело № 389 от 2026 г.": act number 112 in volume III, entry 4567 in the general register, case file 389 of 2026. Book 4 contains the **entered** acts; if the registration judge refuses the entry and the regional court confirms the refusal, the court decision is attached to the act ([[n32:6:2]]). The volumes are numbered, laced, sealed and closed with the signature of the notary ([[n32:6:3]]).

## Notarial case files

A notarial case file ({{нотариално дело}}) is opened for each request for ([[n32:10:1]]):

1. a notarial deed;
2. a notarial will or a revocation of a will, a deposit of a holographic will or of documents;
3. a circumstantial verification (ownership by prescription).

No new file is opened in three cases: a deed that follows a circumstantial verification goes into the file of the verification; a deed for a member of a housing cooperative goes into the file of the cooperative; a revocation of a notarial will made by the same notary goes into the file of that will ([[n32:10:2]]).

The file contains the request, one copy of the signed deed, the declarations of the parties about the special requirements of the law, and the documents that prove the representative power of the parties ([[n32:11:1]]). It also contains the accounting documents for the fees ([[n32:11:4]]). The other documents are returned to the person who brought them, on request. A person who presents an official document must give the notary a copy of it on request ([[n32:11:5]]).

The cover shows the type and number of the file, the parties, the dates of opening and closing, the number in the general register, and the volume and number of the deed ([[n32:10:3]]). Numbering starts each year: one series for deeds and circumstantial verifications, and a separate series for wills and deposits ([[n32:10:4]]).

| Case file | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Deeds and circumstantial verifications | 5 years, then destroyed after agreement with the State Archives |
| Wills, deposits, and decisions in circumstantial verifications | 100 years, then to the State Archives |

Source: [[n32:10:5]].

## The seal

- The seal is **round, 40 mm** in diameter ([[n32:14:1]]).
- In the centre: the first and family name of the notary as in the register, the words "Район на действие", the abbreviation "Р. С." (district court) and the seat of the district court.
- Around the centre: in the upper half, "Нотариус" and the number of the notary in the register; in the lower half, "Република България".
- A notary may have one rubber, one metal and one dry (embossing) seal of the approved model ([[n32:14:3]]).
- If a seal is lost, the notary informs the Notary Chamber at once and a new seal is made. A damaged seal is destroyed by a commission of the Chamber, with a protocol that contains at least five sample prints ([[n32:16]]).

The endorsements ({{удостоверителни надписи}}) on documents may be applied with **rubber stamps** of the prescribed model. Stamps may also carry the additions "помощник" and "по заместване" ([[n32:17]]).

> [!warn] The seal never leaves the control of the notary
> A clerk who finds the seal on an empty desk puts it in the locked drawer. A seal in the wrong hands can produce false certifications that look real.

## Paper and format

- Drafts of notarial deeds are prepared on white A4 sheets of 80 g/m² or more ([[n32:18:1]]).
- A deed that must be entered is prepared in **at least five identical copies**. A notarial will or a revocation is prepared in **at least four** ([[n32:18:1]]).
- The margins must allow binding. The second and every next page are numbered. The text is written on horizontal lines inside a frame on each page ([[n32:18:2]]).
- After the notary's name, the deed states the district, the notary's register number and the town of the office ([[n32:18:4]]).
- All documents use both sides of the sheet. The last page must leave room for the signatures, the endorsements and the notary's signature and seal. If there is no room, an extension sheet ({{алонж}}) is glued on and fixed with the signature and seal in at least two places ([[n32:19:1]], [[n32:19:2]]).
- A document that must be entered has room on the first page, above the title, for the entry data of the registry office ([[n32:19:3]]).
- Multi-page documents and copies are numbered and attached; every two sheets are joined with the signature and seal in at least two places. The copies for the parties may be laced with a cord whose ends are fixed with wax or another material that carries the seal ([[n32:20]]).

## Sending acts for entry

The notary presents the acts for entry on the day they are made, in **four identical copies**, one of which may be only signed. The request for entry is prepared in two copies signed by the notary; the proof of payment of the entry fee is attached to one copy, and the other copy, dated and signed by the registration judge, comes back to the notary on the same day ([[n32:21:1]]).

Wills follow a different rule ([[n32:21:2]], [[n32:21:3]]):

- a notarial will or an act revoking a will goes to the registration judge in one copy **within three days**;
- when a holographic will is deposited, the notary informs the registration judge in writing about the name of the testator **within three days**.

## The information system of the Notary Chamber

The Notary Chamber runs an information system that is part of the archive of every notary ([[znnd:28б]], [[n32:1а]]). The Chamber calls it **"Единство 2"** (Unity 2) on its site, edinstvo.notary-chamber.bg. The access rules are in Instruction No. И-1 of 14 April 2016, which the Chamber publishes with its regulations. The notary must enter **at once** ([[n32:9а]]):

| What | Example |
|---|---|
| Extracts of powers of attorney with signature and content certified at the same time, and of documents that revoke them | Power of attorney to sell an apartment |
| Names and identity numbers of testators: notarial wills, revocations, deposited, returned or announced holographic wills | A will made in the office |
| Extracts of documents with certified signatures that go to the Commercial Register or the Central Register of Special Pledges | Decision of the owner of an EOOD |
| Extracts of powers of attorney for acts before banks, and their revocations | Power of attorney to manage a bank account |
| Scans of contracts for the transfer of motor vehicles; the notary keeps a copy of the contract, the documents of representation and the declarations for two years | Sale of a car |
| Extracts of powers of attorney for vehicles, for the National Revenue Agency, for investment intermediaries, for insurers, and, from 3 September 2027, for electronic administrative services | Power of attorney to register a car |

Banks, insurers and other entities with a legal interest can get paid inquiries from the system; state bodies have free access for official purposes ([[n32:2б]], [[n32:2в]]). The purpose is simple: before a bank or a notary acts on a power of attorney, they can check that it exists and has not been revoked.

## Inquiries and copies from the archive

Information from the case files is given **only** to the parties, their successors and their legal or authorised representatives. A representative who is not a lawyer needs an **express** power of attorney with a notarially certified signature ([[znnd:28а:4]], [[n32:2]]).

## Extra services, bills and accounts

- Extra services under [[znnd:22]], such as drafting documents or obtaining certificates, are agreed in a **written contract** ([[n32:12]]).
- For each fee the office issues a **bill** ({{сметка}}) in two or more copies, signed by the notary. It states the provisions on which the fees are based, the material interest or the time spent, the amounts, the advance received and the consequences of non-payment ([[znnd:89]], [[n32:13:2]]).
- The accounts follow the Accounting Act ([[n32:13:1]]).

> [!task] The clerk's closing checklist
> 1. Every act of the day is in the general register, with no blank lines.
> 2. The notary has signed and sealed the day's entries.
> 3. Every deed for entry went to the registry office, and the dated copy of the request came back.
> 4. Every power of attorney with certified content, every revocation and every vehicle contract is in the information system.
> 5. Every case file has the request, the signed copy, the declarations, the proof of representation and the bill.
> 6. The seals, the wills and the deposits are locked.
