## What a contract is

A contract is an agreement between two or more persons to create, regulate or end a legal relationship between them ([[zzd:8]]). The parties are free to decide its content, as long as it does not contradict the mandatory rules of the law and good morals ([[zzd:9]]). They must act in good faith during the negotiations, or they owe damages ([[zzd:12]]).

A contract is concluded when the acceptance reaches the person who made the offer ([[zzd:14]]). In a notary office, the parties sign the same text in front of the notary at the same time, so the question of offer and acceptance rarely matters. It matters for the **preliminary contract** that the parties made before they came to the office.

## Form

| Contract | Required form | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer of ownership or creation of real rights in real estate | Notarial deed | [[zzd:18]] |
| Mortgage | Notarial deed | [[zzd:167]] |
| Preliminary contract for a deal that needs notarial form | Written form with the essential terms | [[zzd:19]] |
| Power of attorney for a deal in notarial form | Signature and content certified at the same time | [[zzd:37]] |
| Gift of movable property | Written with notarially certified signatures, or by handing over the thing | [[zzd:225]] |
| Transfer of shares in an OOD | Written with signatures and content certified at the same time | [[tz:129:2]] |
| Sale of a motor vehicle | Written with notarially certified signatures | [[zdvp:144:2]] |
| Consent to delete a mortgage | Notarially certified form | [[zzd:179]] |
| Lease of real estate for more than one year | Signatures certified so that it can be entered ([[pv:3]], [[pv:4]]). An entered lease binds a new owner for the whole term; a lease with a certain date but not entered binds the new owner for at most one year after the transfer | [[zzd:237]] |

A contract without the form that the law requires is void ([[zzd:26:1]]).

## Sham deals and the price declaration

Parties sometimes write a different deal from the real one: a "sale" that is really a gift, or a sale at a low price to pay less tax and fees. The law calls this a **sham** contract ({{привидна сделка}}). The sham contract is void ([[zzd:26:1]]). The hidden contract applies if it meets its own conditions of validity ([[zzd:17]]).

To limit this practice, the parties to every onerous deal with real estate **declare in the deed that the price is the payment actually agreed** ([[znnd:25:9]]). A false declaration before the notary is a crime under Art. 313 of the Criminal Code. The notary must explain this to the parties.

> [!warn] A low price does not save money in the end
> Fees and the local tax are calculated on the higher of the price and the tax assessment anyway ([[znnd:96:3]], [[zmdt:46:2]]). A false low price creates risk for the buyer: if the deal is later annulled, the buyer can recover only the price in the deed ([[zzd:34]]).

## Creditors who are harmed

A creditor may ask the court to declare **ineffective towards the creditor** the acts by which the debtor harms the creditor, if the debtor knew about the harm; for an onerous act, the other party must also have known ([[zzd:135]]). When the other party is the spouse, a descendant, an ascendant, a brother or a sister of the debtor, the law presumes that knowledge until the contrary is proved ([[zzd:135]]). This is the Paulian action ({{Павлов иск}}). Typical case: a debtor donates an apartment to a child to avoid enforcement. The notary cannot know every debt, but staff should notice unusual gifts made in a hurry.

## The preliminary contract

A preliminary contract ({{предварителен договор}}) obliges the parties to conclude a final contract later. If the final contract needs notarial form, the preliminary contract must be **written** and must contain the **essential terms** of the final contract ([[zzd:19]]). Each party may sue for the conclusion of the final contract. The final contract is then concluded when the court decision becomes final ([[zzd:19]]). The claim is entered in the property register, which protects the buyer against later deals by the seller ([[pv:11]]).

Two tools often appear in preliminary contracts:

- **Earnest money** ({{задатък}}) proves that the contract exists and secures it. If the party who gave it fails to perform, the other party may withdraw and keep it. If the party who received it fails, the other party may withdraw and claim **double** the amount ([[zzd:93]]).
- **Penalty** ({{неустойка}}) is an agreed amount of damages without proof of the damage. A court may reduce an excessive penalty ([[zzd:92]]).

> [!tip] Why parties certify signatures on a preliminary contract
> The law requires only simple written form. But a certification of the signatures proves who signed and on which date, which matters if one party later denies it. The fee is 30% of the fee under item 8 of the tariff, at least 30 leva (15.34 euro) ([[tarifa:10]]).

## Performance and termination

If one party to a bilateral contract does not perform, the other may terminate it after a written warning with a reasonable term ([[zzd:87]]). **Contracts that transfer, create, recognise or end real rights in real estate can be terminated only by a court** ([[zzd:87]]). The right to terminate lapses after five years. The claim is entered in the property register ([[pv:11]]).

## Limitation periods

As a general rule, claims lapse after **five years** ([[zzd:110]]). Claims for damages and penalties for breach of contract, rent, interest and other periodic payments lapse after **three years** ([[zzd:111]]). This explains why banks and landlords serve notarial invitations in time (chapter 16).

## The main contracts in the office

### Sale

The seller transfers ownership of a thing or another right, and the buyer pays a price ([[zzd:183]]).

- The **costs of the contract** and of the transfer are for the buyer; for **real estate**, the parties pay them **equally**, unless they agree otherwise ([[zzd:186]]).
- The seller is liable if third persons have rights over the thing that they can assert against the buyer, unless the buyer knew of them ([[zzd:188]]).
- The seller is liable for hidden defects that substantially reduce the value or usability; an agreement that excludes this liability is invalid ([[zzd:193]]). The buyer's claims lapse in **one year** for real estate and six months for movables, from delivery ([[zzd:197]]).

### Exchange

The rules on sale apply: each party is a seller of what it gives and a buyer of what it receives ([[zzd:223]]). The local tax is due on the property of higher value ([[zmdt:47:2]]).

### Gift

- A gift transfers something **at once** and **free of charge**; the recipient accepts it ([[zzd:225]]).
- A **promise** of a gift has no effect. A gift of **future** property is void ([[zzd:226]]).
- A gift can be **revoked** only if the recipient tries to kill the donor or close family, falsely accuses the donor of a serious crime, or refuses support that the donor needs. The claim must be filed within **one year** after the donor learns of the ground; if the donor dies within that year, the heirs may file it. A waiver in advance is void ([[zzd:227]]).

### Loan

The lender gives money or other fungible things into the ownership of the borrower, who returns the same amount. Interest is due only if agreed **in writing** (this does not apply to banks) ([[zzd:240]]).

### Assignment of a claim

A creditor may transfer a claim with its securities ([[zzd:99]]). The assignment is effective towards the debtor and third persons only after the **old creditor notifies the debtor** ([[zzd:99]]). Collection agencies often notify debtors through a notarial invitation.

### Mortgage

- A mortgage secures a claim on real estate, for one's own or somebody else's debt ([[zzd:149]]).
- It arises by **entry** in the property register, on the basis of a contract or the law. It covers individually identified properties and a fixed sum of money ([[zzd:166]]).
- The contract is made by **notarial deed** that names the creditor, the debtor, the owner, the property and the secured claim ([[zzd:167]]).
- The entry is effective for **10 years** and can be renewed before it expires ([[zzd:172]]).
- The creditor has priority from the price of the property, whoever owns it ([[zzd:173]]).
- The entry is deleted on the creditor's **consent in notarially certified form** or on a final court decision ([[zzd:179]]).

### Transfer of property against maintenance and care

This contract ({{договор за прехвърляне на имот срещу задължение за издръжка и гледане}}) is not named in ЗЗД. It is valid under the freedom of contract ([[zzd:9]]) and is entered like a sale ([[pv:4]]). An older person transfers a home to a younger person who must support and care for the transferor for life. Because the obligation is personal and long, the contract fails in practice when the relationship fails. The transferor can terminate it only through a court ([[zzd:87]]). Chapter 18 explains the deed.

## Court claims that the registry office records

The following claims about deeds that are entered must themselves be entered or noted in the property register ([[pv:11]]):

- termination under [[zzd:87]];
- annulment under [[zzd:32]];
- revocation of a gift under [[zzd:227]];
- a declaration of nullity under [[zzd:26]];
- a claim for the conclusion of a final contract under [[zzd:19]].

The entry warns every later buyer. When the office checks a property, a recorded claim is one of the most important findings (chapter 17).
