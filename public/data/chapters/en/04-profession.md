## A free profession with a state function

The state assigns notarial acts to the notary ([[znnd:2:1]]), but the notary is not paid by the state and **the state is not liable for the acts of the notary** ([[znnd:73:2]]). The notary earns notarial fees, which the law defines as the payment for the service ([[znnd:§1]]), keeps accounts ([[znnd:33]]) and is socially insured like other free professions ([[znnd:31]]).

The notary is **independent** and obeys only the law ([[znnd:17:1]]). Everybody owes the notary assistance and respect in the exercise of the functions ([[znnd:18]]). When a notary is detained or charged with a crime subject to public prosecution, the Minister of Justice and the Council of Notaries must be informed ([[znnd:17:2]]).

## The district and the office

- The district of a notary is the district of a district court ([[znnd:3]]).
- A notary **cannot perform notarial acts outside the district** ([[gpk:573:1]]). An act made outside the district is null ([[gpk:576]]).
- A notary has **one office** in the district ([[znnd:13:3]]) and must inform the president of the district court at once about any move of the office or the archive ([[znnd:29]]).
- Notaries may work together in a civil partnership, but each keeps a separate archive ([[znnd:34а]]).
- At the notary's reasoned request, the Minister of Justice may move the notary to another district for important reasons, such as health or family circumstances, when a place is open there. The Minister decides within 14 days after an opinion of the Council of Notaries ([[znnd:34б:1]], [[znnd:34б:4]]).

## The duties of the notary

### Towards every person who asks for help

| Duty | What it means in the office | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Consider every request | The notary must deal with each request, unless the notary has an interest in the act or a special relation with the party that raises a well-founded doubt about impartiality. | [[znnd:23]] |
| Impartiality | The notary does not take sides. The notary does not represent the buyer or the seller. | [[znnd:24]] |
| Protect the rights of the parties | Guide the parties, clarify their will and the facts, explain the legal consequences clearly, avoid omissions and delays. | [[znnd:25:1]] |
| Meet alone | The notary meets the person alone, unless the person wants others to be present. This protects people under pressure. | [[znnd:20]] |
| Act personally | The notary performs the acts personally. | [[znnd:2:3]] |
| Professional secrecy | The notary keeps secret all facts learned through the work and does not use them for any benefit, also after the activity ends. | [[znnd:26]] |
| Legality | No notarial act about deals or documents that are against the law or good morals. | [[gpk:574]] |

> [!tip] What "explain the consequences" looks like
> Before a sale with a reserved right of use, the notary explains to the seller that the buyer becomes the owner at once and that the seller keeps only the right to live in the property. Before a gift, the notary explains that a gift can be revoked only in the cases of the law. The clerk does not give legal explanations; the clerk passes questions to the notary or the assistant.

### Checks before an act

The law adds specific checks to the general duty of care:

- **Measures against illegally acquired assets.** Before each act, the notary checks whether a court has imposed a security measure under the law on the forfeiture of illegally acquired assets ([[znnd:25:2]]).
- **National registers.** For every certification about rights in real estate, the notary checks the National Population Database and the National Register of Bulgarian Identity Documents ([[znnd:25:3]], [[znnd:19:2]]).
- **Public debts.** Before a transfer of real rights in real estate, a mortgage or a pledge, the notary checks electronically at the National Revenue Agency that the transferor has no unpaid public debts subject to enforcement ([[dopk:264:1]]). For vehicles, the check covers also the paid local tax on the vehicle ([[dopk:264:2]]). Chapter 21 shows what to do when the check finds a debt.
- **Ownership.** For deeds about real estate, the notary checks that the transferor is the owner and that the special requirements for the deal are met ([[gpk:586:1]]).
- **Money laundering.** The notary is an obliged person under the anti-money laundering law. Chapter 27 explains the checks.

### Declarations that the parties make

- For **dispositions** (sales, gifts, mortgages and so on), the parties declare their citizenship and marital status on a form approved by the Minister of Justice ([[znnd:25:8]]).
- For **onerous deals** with real rights in real estate, the participants declare in the deed that the amount stated is the payment actually agreed ([[znnd:25:9]]).
- Payments under such deals that total **more than 10,000 leva** (5,112.92 euro) go through a special bank account of the notary or a bank account in a bank chosen by the parties ([[znnd:25:10]]).

### Entry in the property register

The notary presents the acts that must be entered to the registry office **on the day** on which the act is made. Before the entry, the notary does not give the parties copies of the act ([[znnd:25:5]]). Acts for property in another district go there through the registration judge, at the cost of the parties ([[znnd:25:6]]).

### The special bank account

For deals with real rights in real estate, the price may be paid into a **special bank account in the name of the notary** ([[znnd:25а:1]]). A written agreement between the notary and the parties sets the conditions for the deposit and for the payment out ([[znnd:25а:2]]). The creditors of the notary cannot enforce against the money in this account ([[znnd:25а:3]]). In practice the buyer pays before the signing and the notary pays the seller after the deed is entered.

## The rights of the notary

- Free access to courts and administrative offices, to case files and documents, with priority ([[znnd:19:1]]).
- Access to the National Population Database and the National Register of Bulgarian Identity Documents ([[znnd:19:2]], [[nnbd:1]], [[nnrbld:1]]).
- An **inviolable archive**: nobody may access it without the consent of the notary, except where a law allows it ([[znnd:21:1]]). Files leave the office only on a written order of a judge or a prosecutor ([[znnd:28:3]]).
- Other paid work related to notarial proceedings: drafting and checking documents, consultations, mediation to clarify the will of the parties, obtaining documents, acting as executor of a will or as manager of property ([[znnd:22]]).

## The Notary Chamber

All notaries are members of the Notary Chamber, a legal entity with its seat in Sofia. There is a notarial college for the district of each court of appeal, and a separate college for Sofia city ([[znnd:4]]).

| Body | Composition and term | Main powers |
|---|---|---|
| General Assembly ({{Общо събрание}}) | All members; regular meeting every year at the end of February ([[znnd:54]], [[znnd:55]]) | Adopts the statute; elects the other bodies for three years; sets the insurance amounts, contributions and budget ([[znnd:57]]) |
| Council of Notaries ({{Съвет на нотариусите}}) | At least five members and two reserves ([[znnd:62]]) | Runs the Chamber, keeps the register, supervises notaries, starts disciplinary cases, organises training ([[znnd:65]]) |
| Control Council ({{Контролен съвет}}) | At least three members ([[znnd:69]]) | Controls the finances of the Chamber ([[znnd:70]]) |
| Disciplinary Commission ({{Дисциплинарна комисия}}) | At least six members ([[znnd:71]]) | Decides disciplinary cases against notaries and assistant notaries ([[znnd:72]]) |

The register of the Chamber lists notaries and assistant notaries and the facts about them, including the district, the office address and the insurer ([[znnd:5]], [[znnd:16]]). Everybody may inspect it and get an extract ([[znnd:7]]). Changes must be reported **within seven days** ([[znnd:16:2]]). The Chamber also runs the information system that forms part of the archive of each notary ([[znnd:28б]]).

## Liability

### Civil liability and insurance

The notary is liable for damage caused by **culpable** non-performance of duties under the general rules of the Obligations and Contracts Act, **but not more than the certified material interest** ([[znnd:73:1]]). The notary must have insurance for the whole time of practice. It covers the faults of the notary, of the assistant notary and of the employees ([[znnd:30:1]]). A copy of each policy goes to the Chamber within seven days ([[znnd:30:2]]). The General Assembly sets the minimum and maximum insured amounts and may create a guarantee fund ([[znnd:57]]).

> [!example] How a clerk's mistake becomes the notary's liability
> A clerk compares an identity card with the register too quickly. A person with a forged card sells an apartment that belongs to someone else. The real owner sues. The court looks at whether the office did the checks that the law requires. If it did not, the notary pays, through the insurance, up to the price in the deed.

### Disciplinary liability

The notary and the assistant notary bear disciplinary liability for culpable breaches of the law and of the statute of the Chamber ([[znnd:74:1]]).

| Penalty | Erased after |
|---|---|
| Reprimand ({{порицание}}) | 1 year |
| Fine from 1,000 to 5,000 leva (511.29 to 2,556.46 euro), paid to the Chamber | 2 years from payment |
| Warning of deprivation of capacity | 3 years |
| Deprivation of capacity for 3 months to 5 years | 5 years after the end of the term |

Sources: [[znnd:75]], [[znnd:80а]].

The procedure, step by step:

1. The Minister of Justice proposes, or the Council of Notaries decides, to open a case ([[znnd:77:1]]).
2. The notary may give explanations within **seven days** ([[znnd:77:2]]).
3. A panel of three members of the Disciplinary Commission hears the case in a closed meeting; the notary may have a lawyer ([[znnd:77:4]], [[znnd:78]]).
4. The panel decides within **one month** of the assignment with a reasoned decision; a deprivation of capacity needs the votes of two thirds of the members ([[znnd:79:3]], [[znnd:79:4]]).
5. The notary, the Council or the Minister may appeal to the Supreme Court of Cassation within **14 days** ([[znnd:80:1]]).

The liability lapses **six months** after the discovery of the breach and in any case **two years** after it was committed ([[znnd:76:1]]).

### Control by the Ministry of Justice

The Minister of Justice controls each notary. Inspectors of the Ministry and **inspector-notaries** do joint inspections ([[znnd:80б:1]]). An inspection starts by an order of the Minister, on the Minister's own initiative, after a complaint, or at the proposal of the Council ([[znnd:80б:2]]). Inspector-notaries have more than 10 years of legal experience, three of them as a notary ([[znnd:80в:2]]).

## Loss of capacity and the archive

A notary loses the capacity on request, on death or judicial disability, when a requirement of [[znnd:8]] fails or an incompatibility arises, or by the disciplinary penalty of deprivation of capacity ([[znnd:35]]). From that moment:

- the notary may not perform any act, and the archive is **sealed** by order of the president of the district court, with a protocol and an inventory ([[znnd:36]]);
- the Council of Notaries deletes the notary from the register and writes who received the archive ([[znnd:37]]);
- the notary or registration judge who receives the archive performs acts only on the documents in it, for example issues copies ([[znnd:38]]).

The capacity comes back on the notary's request in two cases: after a deprivation for a set term, when the term ends; and after an incompatible activity under [[znnd:9:1:1|Art. 9(1), item 1]], when the activity ends. The notary asks the Council of Notaries within **one month** after the ground falls away. The restoration is noted in the register of the Chamber, and the person who holds the archive hands it back with an inventory ([[znnd:39]]).
