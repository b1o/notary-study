## The draft

Before the signing, the office prepares a **draft** of the deed ({{проект}}) in **two or more identical copies**, on the paper and in the format approved by the Minister of Justice ([[gpk:578:1]]). For a deed that must be entered, Ordinance No. 32 requires **at least five** identical copies ([[n32:18:1]]): one for the notarial book, the others for the registry office and the parties.

Rules for the draft:

- All copies are clean and legible, written by hand in black or blue ink, or printed ([[gpk:578:2]]).
- **Numbers are also written in words** when they concern the content of the deal: the price, the area, the shares ([[gpk:578:3]]). Example: "за сумата от 120 000 (сто и двадесет хиляди) евро".
- **Empty spaces are crossed out** ([[gpk:578:3]]).
- The notary's name, district, register number and town appear after the notary's name ([[n32:18:4]]).
- There is room above the title for the data of the registry office ([[n32:19:3]]).

### Content that the law requires

A notarial deed contains ([[gpk:580]]):

1. the year, month and day, and when necessary the **hour and place** of issue;
2. the **name of the notary** who issues it;
3. the **full names and identity numbers** of the participants, and the number, date, place and issuing authority of their **identity documents**;
4. the **content** of the act;
5. a short description of the documents that prove the requirements of [[gpk:586:1]] (ownership and special requirements);
6. the **signatures and handwritten full names** of the parties or their representatives, and the signature of the notary.

A deed that is entered must also contain the addresses of the parties, the **description of the property** (type, location, number, area and boundaries) and the **price** or the certified material interest ([[pv:6:1]]). For companies, the deed states the firm and the ЕИК ([[pv:6:1]]).

> [!warn] What makes a deed null
> Missing items 1, 3, 4 or 6 of [[gpk:580]] make the deed null ([[gpk:576]]). An error in the identity number or in the description of the property does not always make the deed null, but it can stop the entry and force a correction deed. Check the draft against the identity documents and the cadastral data before the parties come.

## The anatomy of a sale deed

```doc Skeleton of a sale deed (simplified)
НОТАРИАЛЕН АКТ
за покупко-продажба на недвижим имот
№ ..., том ..., рег. № ..., дело № ... от 2026 г.

Днес, ... 2026 г., пред мен, [име], нотариус в района на Районен съд – [град], рег. № ... на Нотариалната камара, в канцеларията ми в гр. ..., се явиха:
1. [продавач – три имена, ЕГН, документ за самоличност, адрес], от една страна като ПРОДАВАЧ, и
2. [купувач – същите данни], от друга страна като КУПУВАЧ,
които след като се убедих в самоличността, дееспособността и представителната им власт, заявиха, че сключват следния договор:

I. Продавачът продава на купувача следния свой собствен недвижим имот: [вид, идентификатор по кадастралната карта, адрес, площ, съседи, принадлежности, идеални части от общите части и от правото на строеж].
II. Цената е ... (словом) евро, която купувачът плаща ... [начин на плащане].
III. Страните декларират, че посочената цена е действително уговореното плащане (чл. 25, ал. 9 ЗННД).
IV. [Други уговорки: предаване на владението, тежести, разноски.]

Нотариусът провери, че продавачът е собственик на имота, и че са налице особените изисквания за сделката, като се представиха: [документ за собственост], [скица], [удостоверение за данъчна оценка], [декларации] ...

Този акт се прочете на страните, които след като го одобриха, го подписаха пред мен.

ПРОДАВАЧ: ..........     КУПУВАЧ: ..........
НОТАРИУС: ..........
```

## The signing, step by step

1. **Personal appearance.** The persons whose statements the deed contains, or their agents, appear **personally** before the notary ([[gpk:578:4]]).
2. **Identity, capacity, representation.** The notary checks them before issuing the deed. Persons the notary does not know show an identity document; without one, two witnesses prove the identity ([[gpk:578:4]], [[gpk:578:5]]). Chapter 7 explains the checks.
3. **Ownership and special requirements.** For deeds that transfer ownership or create, transfer, change or end other real rights in real estate, the notary checks that the transferor is the owner and that the special requirements for the deal are met ([[gpk:586:1]]). See the next section.
4. **Reading.** The notary **reads the content of the deed** to the participants ([[gpk:579:1]]).
5. **Signing.** If they approve it, the participants write their **full name** by hand and sign before the notary. If they signed before, they write their full name and confirm their signatures ([[gpk:579:1]]).
6. **The notary signs and seals** and enters the act in the registers.
7. **Entry.** The notary presents the deed for entry on the same day ([[znnd:25:5]]), in four copies with a request in two copies ([[n32:21:1]]).
8. **Copies.** After the issue and the entry, one copy is filed in the special book; the other copies, charged as copies, go to the participants ([[gpk:581]]). Before the entry, the notary gives the parties no copy ([[znnd:25:5]]).

### Special participants

- A participant who cannot sign because of illiteracy or a disability puts the print of the **right thumb**; the deed is **not countersigned** by witnesses ([[gpk:579:2]], [[gpk:189]]).
- A participant who does not know Bulgarian signs through an **interpreter** appointed by the notary, when the notary does not know the participant's language ([[gpk:582]]).
- For deaf, mute or deaf-mute participants, the rules of [[gpk:583]] apply.
- Witnesses and interpreters promise to tell the truth and sign the deed ([[gpk:585]]). They must not fall under [[gpk:584]].

### Corrections

When the deed needs **corrections, additions or deletions**, an express note is made and signed **like the deed itself** ([[gpk:579:3]]). After the deed is signed and entered, an error is corrected only by a **correction deed** ({{акт за поправка}}) signed by the same parties; the tariff charges a fixed fee of 30 leva (15.34 euro) for it ([[tarifa:1]]).

## The ownership check

The notary checks that the transferor is the owner ([[gpk:586:1]]):

- **With documents.** Ownership is proved by the relevant documents ([[gpk:586:2]]): an earlier notarial deed, a court decision, a certificate of heirs with the deed of the deceased, a decision of a land commission for agricultural land, a municipal or state document, and others.
- **Without documents.** If the transferor has no documents, ownership is checked by the procedure of [[gpk:587:2]] (the circumstantial verification, below) ([[gpk:586:2]]).
- **Statement in the deed.** The notary certifies the check in the deed and lists the documents of ownership and the other requirements ([[gpk:586:3]]).
- **Unentered title.** If the transferor's document of ownership is **not entered**, the deed is **not issued** until that document is entered ([[gpk:586:4]]).

"Special requirements" are the conditions that specific laws add: the declarations under [[znnd:25:8]] and [[znnd:25:9]], the tax assessment and the check for public debts ([[dopk:264:1]]), the offer to co-owners ([[zs:33]]), the consent of the spouse, the court permission for children's property ([[sk:130:3]]), the anti-money laundering declarations, and others. Chapter 21 lists them for a sale. Chapter 10 gives the planning rules among them, and chapter 11 the documents of state and municipal property.

## Declaratory deeds of ownership

A person who owns real estate but has **no document** can obtain a **declaratory notarial deed** ({{констативен нотариален акт}}) in two ways ([[gpk:587]]):

### 1. By documents

The owner proves the right before the notary with **proper written evidence** ([[gpk:587:1]]). Example: a house built in the 1970s on a plot that the owner bought by deed, with a building permit and an act of the municipality that the building was accepted.

### 2. By circumstantial verification

If the owner has no written evidence, or the evidence is not enough, the notary performs a **circumstantial verification** ({{обстоятелствена проверка}}) for acquisition by **prescription** ([[gpk:587:2]]):

1. The owner files a written request-declaration ({{молба-декларация}}) with a sketch of the property and its tax assessment ([[gpk:571]]).
2. The fee is paid with the request and is **not returned** if ownership is not recognised ([[znnd:88:1]], [[tarifa:11]]).
3. The **mayor** of the municipality, district or village where the property is, or an official he designates, **names three witnesses** at the owner's suggestion. As far as possible they should be **neighbours** of the property ([[gpk:587:2]]).
4. The notary **questions the witnesses** about the facts of possession: since when, how, without interruption, as an owner. A protocol is drawn up and kept in the case file ([[n32:11:3]]).
5. The notary decides by a **reasoned decision** ({{постановление}}). If ownership is recognised, the notary issues the deed ([[gpk:587:3]]).

The declaratory deed contains the notary's data, the owner's full name and identity data, and an **exact description** of the property with its boundaries and location ([[gpk:588:1]]). The rules on personal appearance, reading and copies do not apply to it ([[gpk:588:2]]). It is entered in the property register ([[pv:4]]). The local tax on acquisition is due also for acquisition by prescription ([[zmdt:44:3]]).

> [!note] The value of a declaratory deed
> A declaratory deed **certifies** ownership; it does not create it. It legitimises the holder as owner. A person who disputes it must prove in court that the holder is not the owner. A true owner who was not heard can still win in court.

> [!reform] Stricter rules proposed in 2026
> The Ministry of Justice draft of August 2026 would require the notary to collect written evidence as well as witness statements, allow the notary to require the personal appearance and a declaration of the applicant, announce each procedure publicly on the website and the notice board of the municipality, and set a waiting period after the announcement before the witnesses are questioned. These rules are not law yet.
