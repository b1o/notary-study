## Merchants

A **merchant** ({{търговец}}) is any natural person or legal entity that carries out by occupation one of the commercial deals listed in the Commerce Act, such as buying goods to resell them, banking or construction ([[tz:1]]). Farmers, craftsmen and free professions are not merchants unless their activity is an enterprise ([[tz:2]]). Notaries are a free profession, not merchants.

### Sole trader

Any capable natural person with a domicile in Bulgaria may register as a **sole trader** ({{едноличен търговец}}, ЕТ) ([[tz:56]]). The firm contains the person's name in full ([[tz:59]]). A sole trader is **not** a separate legal entity: the person is liable with all personal property, and in the notary office the person signs personally. The firm can be transferred only together with the enterprise, in the form of [[tz:15:1]] ([[tz:60]]).

### Companies

Commercial companies are legal entities ([[tz:63]]). The Commerce Act knows the following types ([[tz:64]]):

| Type | Abbreviation | Who is liable | Who usually represents |
|---|---|---|---|
| General partnership | СД | All partners, unlimited | Each partner |
| Limited partnership | КД | General partners unlimited, limited partners up to their contribution | The general partners |
| Limited liability company | ООД / ЕООД | Partners only up to their contribution ([[tz:113]]) | The manager ([[tz:141]]) |
| Joint-stock company | АД / ЕАД | Shareholders only up to their shares | Board of directors or management board |
| Partnership limited by shares | КДА | Mixed | General partners |
| Company with variable capital | ДПК | Members up to their contribution | Manager |

The OOD is by far the most common. Its capital may not be less than **1 euro**, and each share not less than one euro cent ([[tz:117:1]]). One person can form it as an EOOD ([[tz:116:2]]).

## The Commercial Register

The Registry Agency keeps the Commercial Register and the register of non-profit legal entities. They are public, and everybody has free access to the database ([[ztr:11:1]]). The registers give **public trust**: third parties in good faith may rely on what is entered or announced, even if it does not exist; facts that are not entered do not exist for third parties in good faith ([[ztr:10]]).

What the office checks for every company:

- the **unified identification code** ({{ЕИК}});
- the firm, the seat and the address;
- the **manager** or the members of the board, and the **way of representation** (alone, jointly, with limits);
- any **procurator** and the scope of the procuration ([[tz:24]]);
- any liquidation, insolvency, or pending registration.

Entries are made on an application ([[ztr:13]]) by the merchant, a procurator, another person in the cases of law, or a lawyer with an express power of attorney ([[ztr:15]]). Many documents for the register need notarially certified signatures: the **specimen signature** and the consent of a manager ([[tz:141:3]]), share transfer contracts ([[tz:129:2]]), and protocols of decisions (below). Notaries must enter extracts of such documents in the information system of the Notary Chamber ([[n32:9а]]).

## Decisions of an OOD and an EOOD

The general meeting of an OOD decides the main matters ([[tz:137:1]]): changes of the articles, admission and expulsion of partners and consent to the transfer of a share to a new member, the annual accounts, the capital, the manager, branches, **acquisition and disposal of real estate**, and others.

> [!law] A protocol with notarial certification
> For decisions on admission or expulsion of partners and consent to a transfer of a share (item 2), on the capital (item 4), on the **election** of the manager (item 5, first proposal) and on the **acquisition or disposal of real estate** (item 7), the protocol is drawn up **with notarial certification of the signatures and content, made at the same time**, unless the articles provide simple written form ([[tz:137:4]]). Decisions taken in breach of this rule are **null** ([[tz:137:5]]).

In an EOOD, the sole owner decides the matters of the general meeting and draws up a protocol **in the form required for the decisions of the general meeting** ([[tz:147:2]]). So the protocol of a sole owner who decides to sell the company's real estate also needs this form, unless the articles say otherwise.

Majorities: decisions under items 1, 2 and 9 need more than three quarters of the capital; decisions on the capital need unanimity; other decisions need more than half of the capital, unless the articles say otherwise ([[tz:137:3]]).

## Transfer of a company share

- A share ({{дружествен дял}}) can be transferred and inherited. A transfer between partners is free. A transfer to a **third person** needs the admission of a new partner by the general meeting and requires that the company has **no unpaid wages, compensations or mandatory social contributions** of its workers, including those who left in the last three years ([[tz:129:1]]).
- **Form:** a contract with notarial certification of the **signatures and content, made at the same time** ([[tz:129:2]]).
- The transfer is entered in the Commercial Register after the manager and the transferor declare that there are no unpaid debts to workers; from 2 April 2027 the registration officer also checks electronically at the National Revenue Agency that the company and the transferor have no unpaid enforceable public debts for social contributions ([[tz:129:2]]).
- A mayor may not certify such signatures ([[znnd:83:2]]).

The fee: when the Commerce Act or the Special Pledges Act requires the content to be certified together with the signatures, the content costs an **ordinary fee** from section I of the tariff, not a fee on the price ([[znnd:90:2]], [[tarifa:4]]). The provision does not name the fee for the signatures; chapter 28 explains the two practices. A declaratory protocol under the Commerce Act costs 50 лв. (25.56 €) ([[tarifa:1]]).

## Transfer of a commercial enterprise

- An enterprise as a whole, with its rights, obligations and factual relations, can be transferred by a written contract with notarial certification of the **signatures and content, made at the same time** ([[tz:15:1]]).
- The transferor must notify the creditors and the debtors ([[tz:15:1]]).
- Unless agreed otherwise with the creditors, the transferor is jointly liable with the acquirer up to the value of the rights received ([[tz:15:3]]).
- The transfer is entered in the Commercial Register for both parties, after a declaration about unpaid wages; from 2 April 2027 also after a check for unpaid social contributions ([[tz:16]]).
- For six months after the entry, the acquirer manages the enterprise separately, and creditors may ask for performance or security ([[tz:16а]]).
- The material interest for the fee is the book value of the fixed assets for the last quarter ([[znnd:97:1]]).

## Representation through procurators and commercial agents

A **procurator** ({{прокурист}}) manages the enterprise and may perform all acts connected with the business, but may not sell or encumber real estate without special authorisation ([[tz:21]], [[tz:22]]). The procuration is given in writing with notarially certified signatures and is entered in the Commercial Register with a specimen of the procurator's signature ([[tz:21:1]]); it is effective towards third parties after the entry ([[tz:24]]). A **commercial agent** ({{търговски пълномощник}}) performs only the acts stated in the power, which is written with a notarially certified signature; real estate, bills of exchange, loans and litigation need an express authorisation ([[tz:26:1]], [[tz:26:2]]). Both powers are notarial work.

## Special pledges

A **special pledge** ({{особен залог}}) is a pledge without handing over the property, for example over machines, receivables, company shares or a whole enterprise ([[zoz:1]], [[zoz:4]]). The pledgor must as a rule be a merchant, with exceptions, for example a pledge of company shares by any person ([[zoz:3]]).

- The pledge arises by **entry** in the Central Register of Special Pledges on the basis of a pledge contract ([[zoz:2:1]]).
- The contract is in **written form**, and in the cases provided in the act, in written form with **notarially certified signatures**. Every consent of the pledgee is given in writing with a **notarially certified signature** ([[zoz:2:2]], [[zoz:2:3]]).
- The pledge is effective against third parties from the entry ([[zoz:12:1]]).

## Bills of exchange and protests

A bill of exchange ({{менителница}}) and a promissory note ({{запис на заповед}}) are securities for payment. When the drawee refuses to accept or to pay, the refusal must be established by a **protest** ([[tz:496]]).

- The protest is made at the **written request** of the holder by the **notary of the place of payment or acceptance** ([[tz:501]]).
- A protest for non-payment of a bill payable on a fixed day must be made within **one of the two working days** after the day of payment ([[tz:498]]).
- The protest contains a full copy of the document, the names of the persons for and against whom it is made, the question to the person, the answer or the note that the person did not answer or was not found, and the place and date ([[tz:502]]).
- The notary enters the content of the protest in the register and issues copies; the original goes to the holder ([[tz:504]]).
- A holder who misses the protest deadlines loses the claims against the endorsers and the drawer ([[tz:514]]). The drawer or an endorser may waive the protest by the clause "без разноски" (without costs) ([[tz:500]]).

The fee for a protest is 50% of the item 8 fee, at least 30 leva (15.34 euro) ([[tarifa:9]]). Chapter 19 explains how the notary visits the debtor.
