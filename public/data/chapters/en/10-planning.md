## Why the office reads planning law

The Spatial Development Act ({{Закон за устройство на територията}}, ЗУТ) regulates planning, design and construction, and it sets the **restrictions on ownership for planning purposes** ([[zut:1:2]]). Many of these restrictions decide whether a deal is possible at all: a part of a plot that is too small cannot be sold, a building cannot be divided without approved projects, a flat under construction can be sold only from a certain stage. They are "special requirements for the deal" that the notary checks before a deed ([[gpk:586:1]]). This chapter shows the rules that come up in deeds.

## Territories and plots

According to their basic purpose, the territories are **urbanised** (settlements and similar areas), **agricultural**, **forest**, **protected**, **damaged** territories for recovery, territories covered by **water**, and territories of **transport** ([[zut:7:1]]). The **specific purpose** of each plot, for example housing, offices or a park, is set by the **detailed development plan** ({{подробен устройствен план}}, ПУП) ([[zut:8]]).

Two terms appear in every file. Both are defined in § 5 of the Additional Provisions of ЗУТ (outside the library extract):

- a **land plot** ({{поземлен имот}}) is a part of the territory with boundaries set by ownership (§ 5, item 2);
- a **regulated land plot** ({{урегулиран поземлен имот}}, УПИ) is a plot for which a detailed development plan set boundaries, access from a street, road or alley, a specific purpose and building rules (§ 5, item 11).

Building ({{застрояване}}) is allowed only if an effective detailed development plan provides for it, and after a change of the purpose of the land when a special law requires it ([[zut:12:2]]). In agricultural, forest and protected plots, objects that suit the purpose of the plot may be built without a change of purpose ([[zut:12:3]]).

> [!note] The cadastral identifier and the regulated plot
> The cadastre gives each land plot an identifier (chapter 29). A regulated plot is a planning unit: one regulated plot can cover one cadastral plot, several of them, or parts of them ([[zut:16:5]], [[zut:17:2]]). Read both the sketch from the cadastre and the plan before you describe a plot in a deed.

## How a plan changes plot boundaries

A detailed development plan regulates streets, blocks and plots ([[zut:14:1]]). A regulated plot must have frontage on a street, a road or, by exception, an alley in a park ([[zut:14:4]]). The regulation lines become the boundaries of the plots only when the plan is **implemented** as to the regulation ([[zut:14:5]]). A plot that was regulated once is not regulated again, except in the cases of the act ([[zut:15:1]]).

Several of these changes need a contract that the notary certifies:

| Change | What the owners sign | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Change of the boundary between regulated plots | An application and a **preliminary contract for the transfer of ownership with notarially certified signatures**; building in the plots is allowed after the **final contract** | [[zut:15:3]], [[zut:15:7]] |
| Completion of a small plot with parts of neighbouring plots, or a new co-owned regulated plot | An application and a preliminary contract with notarially certified signatures, which sets the ideal parts of the co-owners; building after the final contract | [[zut:17:3]], [[zut:17:6]] |
| The same changes for state or municipal plots | A contract in **written form**, at market prices, signed by the regional governor or the mayor | [[zut:15:4]], [[zut:15:5]], [[zut:17:4]], [[zut:17:5]] |
| A co-owned regulated plot in a new plan for an unregulated territory | A joint application of the owners with notarially certified signatures, which sets their ideal parts | [[zut:16:5]] |

## Minimum size and partition of plots

For low residential building, a regulated plot needs at least ([[zut:19:1]]):

| Location | Frontage | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Towns | 14 m | 300 m² |
| Resorts and resort areas | 16 m | 500 m² |
| Villa zones | 18 m | 600 m² |
| Villages on mainly flat ground | 16 m (14 m on main streets or in special conditions) | 500 m² (300 m²) |
| Villages on mainly steep ground | 12 m | 250 m² |

The minimum may be reduced by at most one fifth, on an opinion of the municipal expert council ([[zut:19:3]]). When such a plot is divided, each part must have at least the minimum size reduced by one fifth ([[zut:19:4]]).

The rule that matters most at the desk: **real parts** of plots in settlements can be acquired by a deal or by prescription **only if** they meet the minimum sizes ([[zut:200:1]]). The only exception is a part that joins a neighbouring plot under Art. 17 while the rest of the plot still meets the minimum, or joins another neighbour ([[zut:200:2]]). In a court partition of a regulated plot, the court asks the municipality whether the plot can be divided ([[zut:201:1]], [[zut:201:2]]).

> [!example] A real part or an ideal part?
> Ana owns a regulated plot of 500 m² in a town. She wants to sell 200 m² of it to her neighbour Boris, who will build his own house there. A real part of 200 m² is below the 300 m² minimum, so the deal is not possible ([[zut:200:1]]). Ana can sell Boris an **ideal part** (a share of 2/5) instead. Art. 200 speaks of real parts; a share makes Ana and Boris co-owners ([[zs:30]]), and they then need a notarial contract with each other before either of them builds (see below, [[zut:183:1]]).

For a deal that forms new or changed plots, the office also orders a **sketch-project** from the cadastre ([[zkir:52:1]]).

## Buildings and their parts

- A voluntary **partition of a co-owned building**, dwelling or other object, and a deal that transfers **real parts** of them, are possible only if the parts match **approved investment projects**. The municipality certifies this ([[zut:202]]). The office asks for this certificate before such a deed. The rule does not apply to auxiliary buildings (Art. 147(1), item 1 ЗУТ, outside the extract).
- A building may be **used** only after it is put into service ([[zut:178:1]]): with a **permit for use** issued by the National Construction Control Directorate for buildings of the first to the third category ([[zut:177:2]]), or with a **certificate of commissioning** issued by the authority that issued the building permit for the fourth and fifth category ([[zut:177:3]]). The law does not make the commissioning a condition of a sale, but the buyer should know whether the building may already be used.

### Changes inside a condominium

| Change | What the law requires | Article |
|---|---|---|
| A flat on the ground or semi-basement floor becomes an office, a surgery or a studio with outside visitors | Express written **notarially certified consent** of all owners of dwellings that are direct neighbours of the object | [[zut:38:3]] |
| A ground-floor flat becomes a shop | A decision of the general meeting of the owners and the notarially certified consent of the direct neighbours | [[zut:38:4]] |
| An alteration that takes over common parts | The consent of **all** owners with notarially certified signatures | [[zut:185:2]] |
| A common part joins a flat, or a new object is made from a common part | A **contract for the transfer of ownership in notarial form** with the other owners; the contract is entered | [[zut:185:3]], [[zut:185:7]] |

## Building rights under the Spatial Development Act

The Property Act creates the building right (chapter 9). ЗУТ adds the stages of construction:

- The right to build a building or a part of it may be **transferred from its creation until the rough construction** ({{груб строеж}}) is finished ([[zut:181:1]]).
- **After the rough construction**, the deal transfers the **building or its separate parts** ([[zut:181:2]]).
- The completion of the rough construction is stated by a **protocol**, drawn up by the construction supervisor in the presence of the investor, the builder, the designer and a municipal official ([[zut:181:3]]). In practice the office asks for a copy of this protocol before a deed for a flat.

ЗУТ defines the rough construction as a building with its outer walls and its roof finished, with or without finishing works (§ 5, item 46 of the Additional Provisions, outside the extract).

| Situation | Form | Article |
|---|---|---|
| A new building or an extension in a **co-owned** regulated plot by one or more co-owners | A contract with the other owners **in notarial form** | [[zut:183:1]] |
| Adding floors or an extension to a **condominium** building | A contract for the right to add floors with the owner of the plot **in notarial form**, and a **declaration of consent with notarially certified signatures** of all owners in the condominium | [[zut:183:2]] |
| The state or a municipality is a co-owner of the plot | The contracts are made in **written form** | [[zut:183:3]] |
| A low-rise plot where the other co-owners have built, started or hold rights to build | No consent of the others for the building permit | [[zut:183:4]] |

## Rights of way and networks

| Right | Form | Entry | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| A temporary road through other plots | A written contract between the owners with notarially certified signatures, or an order of the mayor | — | [[zut:190:6]] |
| A right of passage through another plot | A **written contract with notarially certified signatures**; without agreement, an order of the mayor | In the partidas of both plots | [[zut:192:1]], [[zut:192:2]], [[zut:192:7]] |
| A branch of a common network (water, power, sewage) through another property | A written contract between the owners with notarially certified signatures | In the partidas of both plots | [[zut:193:1]], [[zut:193:9]] |

The Supreme Court of Cassation held that a contract for a network branch in this simple form is entered only between owners of two or more properties. It is not entered in favour of a person who owns no other property (Interpretative Decision No. 7 of 25 April 2013, case 7/2012, General Assembly of the Civil and Commercial Colleges; chapter 20).

## What the office checks

| Deal | What the office asks for | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Sale of a real part of a plot | That the part and the rest meet the minimum sizes; a sketch-project | [[zut:19:1]], [[zut:200:1]], [[zkir:52:1]] |
| Voluntary partition of a plot | Minimum size of each part; a sketch-project | [[zut:19:4]], [[zut:200:1]] |
| Partition of a building, or sale of a real part of a building | The certificate of the municipality that the parts match approved projects | [[zut:202]] |
| Sale of a flat in a building under construction | Before the rough construction: the building right for the flat; after it: the flat, with the protocol | [[zut:181]] |
| Building in a co-owned plot | A notarial contract among the co-owners | [[zut:183:1]] |
| Adding floors to a condominium | A notarial deed with the owner of the plot and the certified consents of all owners | [[zut:183:2]] |
| Joining a common part to a flat | A notarial contract with all other owners; entry | [[zut:185:3]], [[zut:185:7]] |
| A right of passage or a network branch | A written contract with certified signatures; entry in both partidas | [[zut:192]], [[zut:193]] |
| A change of plot boundaries by a plan | A preliminary contract with certified signatures; the final contract before building | [[zut:15:3]], [[zut:17:3]] |

> [!task] Practice
> A family owns a house with a yard of 700 m² in a town and wants to (1) give the son a real part of 350 m² with the old house, (2) let a developer add a floor to a neighbouring condominium in which they own a flat, and (3) give a neighbour a right to lay a water pipe through the yard. For each step, name the form, the documents and the provision. Answers: (1) possible if both parts meet 14 m and 300 m² ([[zut:19:1]], [[zut:200:1]]), with a sketch-project ([[zkir:52:1]]); the part becomes a separate regulated plot only when the plan sets its boundaries (§ 5, item 11 ЗУТ); (2) the family signs the declaration of consent with a certified signature, the developer signs the notarial contract with the owners of the plot ([[zut:183:2]]); (3) a written contract with certified signatures, entered in both partidas ([[zut:193:1]], [[zut:193:9]]).
