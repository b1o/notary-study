## Three questions before every act

Before any deed or certification, the notary must check three things about every person whose statement appears in the document: **identity**, **capacity to act** and **representative power** ([[gpk:578:4]]). The persons must appear **personally** before the notary. This chapter explains how the office answers the three questions. A breach of the rule of personal appearance makes the act null ([[gpk:576]]).

## Identity

### With an identity document

The identity of persons whom the notary does not know is established with an **identity document**. The same document shows whether the person is an adult ([[gpk:578:5]]). Bulgarian identity documents are the identity card, the passport (including diplomatic, service and seaman's passports), the military identity card and the driving licence ([[zbld:13:1]]). Foreigners usually present a passport or an identity card of an EU Member State, and a residence document if they have one.

What the office checks:

1. **The face against the photo**, in good light, without hurry.
2. **The document itself**: validity date, damage, signs of manipulation.
3. **The data in the draft**: names exactly as in the document, the identity number ({{ЕГН}}) or the personal number of a foreigner ({{ЛНЧ}}), the number, date, place and issuing authority of the document. A notarial deed must contain all of them ([[gpk:580:3]]).
4. **The national registers.** For certifications about real estate, the notary checks the National Population Database and the National Register of Bulgarian Identity Documents ([[znnd:25:3]]). Only notaries and assistant notaries have individual access ([[nnbd:2:1]], [[nnrbld:2]]). The population database shows, among other things, the address history, **legal restrictions**, the **marital status**, the marriage and divorce, and **data about death** ([[nnbd:3:2]]).

> [!tip] What the registers tell you that the card does not
> A seller presents a valid card and says "I am divorced". The population database shows a marriage with no divorce. The seller's statement about the marital status matters for the community property of spouses (chapter 10). Ask, check the documents, and let the notary decide.

### Without an identity document

If the person has no identity document, the person proves identity with **two witnesses whose identity is established** ([[gpk:578:5]]). The witnesses must meet the conditions of [[gpk:584]]: for example, they cannot be relatives of the parties, beneficiaries of the act, employees of the notary office or employees of the registry office. They promise to tell the truth ([[gpk:585]]).

## Capacity to act

| Person | Capacity | Who signs | What else is needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult (18+) | Full ([[zls:2]]) | The person | Nothing, unless there are doubts |
| Minor 14–18 ({{непълнолетен}}) | Limited ([[zls:4]]) | The minor personally | Consent of a parent or curator; court permission for dispositions of real estate and other important property ([[sk:130:3]]) |
| Child under 14 ({{малолетен}}) | None ([[zls:3]]) | The parents or the guardian, in the child's name | Court permission for dispositions ([[sk:130:3]]) |
| Adult under full judicial disability ({{пълно запрещение}}) | None ([[zls:5]]) | The guardian ({{настойник}}) | Court permission for dispositions; opinion of the guardianship council ([[sk:165:4]]) |
| Adult under limited judicial disability ({{ограничено запрещение}}) | Limited ([[zls:5]]) | The person | Consent of the curator ({{попечител}}); court permission for dispositions ([[sk:168:2]]) |

Two rules protect children absolutely: a **gift**, a **waiver of rights**, a **loan** and **security for somebody else's debt** made by a minor are **void**. Only security through a pledge or a mortgage may be allowed by the court as an exception, for a clear benefit of the child or an extraordinary need of the family ([[sk:130:4]]). Chapter 10 explains the permission of the district court.

### Doubts about the capacity of an adult

An adult who signs without being able to understand or control their acts makes a **voidable** contract ([[zzd:31]]). The notary has no medical training, but the notary must clarify the will of the parties and explain the consequences ([[znnd:25:1]]), and must not perform acts against the law ([[gpk:574]]). In practice:

- the notary talks with the person **alone** ([[znnd:20]]) and asks open questions: what are you signing today, why, what do you get, what happens to your home;
- if the answers are unclear, the notary postpones the act;
- some notaries ask for a recent psychiatric certificate for very old or ill clients. The law does not require it, but it is evidence that the notary acted with care.

> [!warn] Pressure from relatives
> A relative who answers for the client, holds the client's documents and insists on speed is a red flag. Chapter 27 describes the warning signs of property fraud against elderly people.

## Participants with special needs

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Cannot sign because of illiteracy or disability | Signs with the print of the right thumb, under [[gpk:189]]. A notarial deed is **not** countersigned by witnesses ([[gpk:579:2]]). For a private document, two witnesses countersign ([[gpk:189:1]]). |
| Blind but literate | A private document is countersigned by two witnesses ([[gpk:189:2]]). |
| Deaf, mute or deaf-mute, literate | The deaf person reads the document aloud and says whether they agree; the mute person writes by hand that they read it and agree ([[gpk:583:1]]). |
| Deaf, mute or deaf-mute, illiterate | The notary appoints a Bulgarian sign language interpreter and notes this in the act ([[gpk:583:2]], [[gpk:583:3]]). |
| Does not know Bulgarian, and the notary does not know the person's language | The notary appoints an interpreter ([[gpk:582]]). The interpreter promises to translate truly and signs ([[gpk:585]]). |

The persons who **cannot** be witnesses or interpreters are listed in [[gpk:584]]: persons without capacity, persons who are illiterate in Bulgarian, relatives of the parties or of the notary, persons who benefit from the act, blind, deaf and mute persons, and persons who work in the notary office or in the registry office. A sign language interpreter may be a relative of a participant.

## Foreigners

- The capacity of a person follows the law of the state of which the person is a citizen ([[kmchp:50:1]]). A Bulgarian who is also a citizen of another state is treated as Bulgarian ([[kmchp:48:2]]).
- Foreigners have the same civil rights as Bulgarians, unless a law provides otherwise ([[kmchp:49:2]]).
- The main exception is **land**. Foreigners and foreign legal entities may acquire ownership of land only under the conditions of EU accession, an international treaty, or by inheritance by law ([[konst:22]]). Citizens and legal entities of the EU and the EEA may acquire farmland under the conditions of the Agricultural Land Act ([[zspzz:3:5]], [[zspzz:3:6]]). For **every** buyer of farmland, Bulgarian or foreign, that act requires residence or establishment in Bulgaria for more than five years, and an individual buyer declares the origin of the funds before the notary ([[zspzz:3в]]). Buildings and apartments are not land; foreigners may buy them, but not the land under a house unless the conditions apply.

## Companies and other legal entities

### What to check

1. **The Commercial Register** ({{Търговски регистър}}). It is public and free online ([[ztr:11:1]]). Third parties in good faith may rely on what is entered; facts that are not entered do not exist for them ([[ztr:10]]). Print or save the current status ({{актуално състояние}}) on the day of the act.
2. **Who represents the company**:
   - In a limited liability company ({{ООД}}, {{ЕООД}}), the manager ({{управител}}). With several managers, each acts alone unless the articles say otherwise ([[tz:141:2]]).
   - In a single-owner company, the owner decides the matters of the general meeting, with a written protocol ([[tz:147]]).
   - A procurator ({{прокурист}}) may represent the merchant, but may **not** sell or encumber real estate without a special authorisation ([[tz:22:2]]).
3. **Internal decisions** required by law. In an OOD, the general meeting decides on the acquisition and disposal of real estate and real rights ([[tz:137:1]]). The office asks for the protocol of the decision with the signatures certified, as the notary requires.
4. **Beneficial owners** under the anti-money laundering law (chapter 23).

## Representation

Representation exists **by law** (parents, guardians, managers of companies) or **by will** (a power of attorney) ([[zzd:36]]).

### Checking a power of attorney

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Form | If the contract needs a special form, the power of attorney needs the same form; for a notarial deed, a written power with signature and content certified **at the same time** ([[zzd:37]]) |
| Scope | The power covers what the principal stated ([[zzd:39]]). A power "to manage" does not cover "to sell". |
| Self-contracting | The agent may not contract with himself or for another person he also represents, unless the principal agreed ([[zzd:38]]). |
| Still valid | A power ends by revocation, renunciation, death or judicial disability of either side, or the end of a legal entity ([[zzd:41]]). Check the information system of the Notary Chamber for revocations ([[n32:9а]]). |
| Sub-delegation | Allowed only if authorised or necessary to protect the principal ([[zzd:43]]). |
| Foreign power | Apostille or legalisation and a translation (chapter 5). |

The office keeps the documents that prove the representative power in the case file ([[n32:11:1]]).

> [!task] Desk checklist for every person
> 1. Identity document valid and matching the face.
> 2. Names and numbers in the draft exactly as in the document.
> 3. Age, capacity and legal restrictions clear; consents and court permissions present.
> 4. Representation proven: the Commercial Register status of the day, decisions, powers of attorney checked in the system.
> 5. Special needs handled: interpreter, witnesses, sign language.
