# Course audit, 22 October 2026

An independent check of the 28 lessons, after the 4-pass review of 21 October 2026 (`00-method.md` and the chapter notes `01-welcome.md` … `28-cases.md`). This audit does not repeat that review. It uses its results and goes further in five places: the questions and cards that pass 3 rewrote, the statements without a citation, the amounts from tariffs outside the library, the items that the review left open, and the scope of the course against the official exam topics.

## The four questions, in short

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Is the information correct? | Yes, with few exceptions. I found 4 factual errors and 2 incomplete statements in about 1,450 items (28 lessons, 336 questions and 364 cards per language). I fixed all 6 in both languages. Every cited provision that I checked agrees with the imported text. |
| 2. Is there enough to study from? | For a **clerk**: yes. For an **assistant notary** (the exam for substitutes) and for the **competition for notaries**: no. The course covers 26 of the 50 official exam topics fully, 19 in part and 5 not at all, and it teaches almost no case law. See "Verdict on question 2". |
| 3. Does the existing content need additions? | Yes. The largest gaps are the Spatial Development Act (ЗУТ) checks, state and municipal property, the general rules of non-contentious proceedings, and four contracts that the notary certifies. The list per chapter is below. |
| 4. Must new lessons be compiled? | Yes. I propose four lessons (A–D) and two optional ones (E–F), with an outline each. I did not write them. |

## How I worked

1. I read all 28 English lessons and all English questions and cards. A script compared the Bulgarian files with the English files: the counts, the position of the right answer, the cited provisions and every number. They agree in all 28 chapters.
2. I checked about 120 provisions directly in `public/data/laws/acts` with a helper script. These were mainly the provisions behind numbers, terms, amounts and the rules in the rewritten questions.
3. I listed every paragraph in the library with an entry into force after today. There are three: Art. 9а item 11 of Ordinance No. 32 (3 September 2027) and Art. 16(2) and 129(2) ТЗ (2 April 2027). The lessons already state these dates correctly.
4. For facts outside the library I used primary sources where possible (listed under "Sources"). For coverage I used the official lists of exam topics: the appendix to Art. 3(2) of Ordinance No. 36 (competition for notaries, 50 topics) and the appendix to Art. 3(2) of Ordinance No. 2/2003 (exam for substitute assistant notaries, 50 topics). The importer does not import these appendices, so I read them on lex.bg. I also used the 2025 exam programme "Нотариално право" of the Faculty of Law of Sofia University (25 topics).

## Verdict on question 2

**For a clerk: yes, the course is enough to study from.** The daily work of a clerk is identity checks, endorsements, registers, the information system, service of invitations, deeds for entry, fees, AML records and data protection. Chapters 1, 4–8 and 13–28 cover all of it, with the provisions. The few errors that I found are small, and none of them is in a rule that a clerk applies at the desk.

**For an assistant notary and for a notary: no, not alone.** There are three reasons:

1. **Scope.** 24 of the 50 official exam topics are only partly covered or not covered (table above). The missing parts are not marginal: the ЗУТ checks (topics 5, 9, 11), state and municipal property (7, 16), restitution (17) and the general rules of non-contentious proceedings (31) are the basis of the ownership check of Art. 586(1) ГПК.
2. **Case law.** Both exams allow "нормативни актове и съдебна практика на хартиен носител" (Art. 3(3) of each ordinance), and questions are practical cases. The course cites almost no decisions of the Supreme Court of Cassation, for example none of its interpretative decisions on declaratory deeds or on the checks of the registration judge.
3. **The library.** Acts that the exam topics name are not imported: ЗУТ, ЗДС, ЗОС, the restitution acts, ЗЮЛНЦ, the Cooperatives Act, the Merchant Shipping Code (КТК), and Art. 530–541 ГПК. The Registry Agency tariff, the statute and the Ethics Code of the Chamber are also outside the library.

The course is a good base for these two roles. With the four lessons A–D below and the additions per chapter, it would cover about 45 of the 50 topics.

## Proposed new lessons

Each outline gives the sections, the main provisions and the sources. I did not write these lessons. The owner decides which ones to write.

### A. Planning and building rules in deeds (ЗУТ) — exam topics 5, 9, 11, 13, 14

1. Territories and plots: urbanised territories, cadastral plots and regulated plots (УПИ); how a plot gets its regulation.
2. Detailed development plans: what they decide; why a deed must agree with them.
3. Minimum size and partition of plots: real parts of a plot in a settlement are acquired only if the minimum sizes are met; partition by the court and the opinion of the municipality. Art. 19, 200, 201.
4. Buildings: partition of a co-owned building and transfer of real parts only by approved investment projects, certified by the municipality. Art. 202.
5. Building rights in practice: transfer of the right until the rough construction, then of the building or its parts; the protocol of the rough construction. Art. 181. Who may build on another's plot. Art. 182. Building in a co-owned plot (contract in notarial form), adding floors to a condominium (deed with the owner of the plot and a declaration of consent of all owners with certified signatures), written form when the state or a municipality is a co-owner. Art. 183.
6. Servitudes and temporary roads: contract with certified signatures. Art. 190(6); the network servitudes (Art. 192–193, to verify).
7. A checklist for the office.

Sources: ЗУТ on lex.bg (ldoc 2135163904), import of the articles above; exam topics 5, 9, 11 of both ordinances.

### B. State and municipal property, restitution and the history of a title — topics 7, 16, 17, 18, 35

1. Kinds of ownership: public and private, state and municipal. Art. 2–7 ЗС; Art. 2 ЗДС; Art. 2, 3 ЗОС (verified).
2. Acts of state and municipal property: official documents with no constitutive effect; how to read them in a chain of title. Art. 5 ЗДС, Art. 5 ЗОС (verified).
3. Deals with the state and municipalities: who decides, who signs, which form. Art. 183(3) ЗУТ (verified); the implementing regulations of ЗДС and the municipal ordinances (to verify).
4. Prescription and public property: Art. 86 ЗС; the moratorium from 1 June 2006 to 7 March 2022 (§ 1 ЗД ЗС, Decision No. 3 of 2022).
5. Restitution: decisions of the agricultural land offices with the force of a declaratory deed (ЗСПЗЗ: Art. 5(2) in the extract mentions them; the provisions on the decisions themselves are not in the extract and need an import and a check), restitution by law under ЗВСОНИ (to import).
6. Documents with the force of a notarial deed, and the evidence of ownership before the notary.

Sources: ЗДС (ldoc 2133874689), ЗОС (ldoc 2133874691), ЗСПЗЗ (extend the extract), ЗВСОНИ (to import), ЗС (importer change for § 1 of 2006); news.lex.bg, 8 March 2022.

### C. Non-contentious procedure, defects of acts and the entry procedure — topics 31, 32, 34, 40, 41, 42

1. General rules: written request, check by the authority on its own initiative, personal appearance and declarations, suspension when a dispute arises, effect of the act and the claim of a third person, appeal against a refusal within one week, the rules of claim proceedings that apply. Art. 530–541 ГПК (verified; add "530-541" to the `gpk` include list).
2. Defects: null acts (Art. 576), voidable statements, the correction deed, cancellation of the act after a claim (Art. 537(2)). Case law on declaratory deeds (interpretative decisions of the Supreme Court of Cassation; numbers to verify).
3. The entry as a proceeding: the acts subject to entry, notes and deletions, the refusal of the registration judge and the appeal, the books, inquiries and certificates. ПВ Art. 4, 6, 9, 11, 32а, 33, 46, 47; ЗКИР Art. 76–85 (in the library).
4. Evidence and burden of proof before the notary.

Sources: ГПК on lex.bg (ldoc 2135558368); ПВ and ЗКИР (in the library); Sofia University programme, topics 23–25.

### D. Other contracts at the notary — topics 24, 25, 27

1. Assumption of a debt: joining the debtor and replacing the debtor. Art. 101, 102 ЗЗД (verified).
2. Pledge of movables and claims: delivery; a certain date against third persons. Art. 156 ЗЗД (verified), Art. 157–165 (to read); special pledges (chapter 12).
3. Lease and agricultural lease: form, the notary's check of more than 50% of the shares, entry and registration, minimum term of five years. Art. 228–239 ЗЗД; Art. 3, 4 ЗАЗ (verified).
4. Sale of an inheritance: form, entry, effects; material interest. Art. 212–214 ЗЗД (verified); Art. 97(1) item 4 ЗННД.
5. Optional: maritime mortgage and maritime protest (КТК, to import).

Sources: ЗЗД and ЗАЗ (in the library); КТК (to import if chosen).

### E (optional). Non-profit entities, cooperatives and changes of legal entities — topics 2, 3

Representation and decisions of associations, foundations and cooperatives; transformation of companies; the register of non-profit entities. Sources: ЗЮЛНЦ, the Cooperatives Act, Art. 261–264 ТЗ (all to import); ЗТРРЮЛНЦ (in the library).

### F (optional). Cross-border cases — topic 30

One chapter that collects what chapters 3, 5, 7, 10, 11 and 20 now say in parts: the applicable law (КМЧП), the EU regulations on succession (650/2012), on property regimes (2016/1103, 2016/1104) and on public documents (2016/1191), the apostille, and the use of foreign notarial acts in Bulgaria.
